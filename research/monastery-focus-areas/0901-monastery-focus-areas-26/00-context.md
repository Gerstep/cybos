## GP thesis to test (not to rubber-stamp)

AI will **radically change the economy**, but **diffusion takes ~10 years**. Meanwhile **AI capabilities keep compounding at the 2024–2026 rate** (very fast).

Implications to research, not assume:

1. Capabilities >> deployed economic impact for most of the 5-year window.
2. Recursive self-improvement / continual learning, if they land, change the slope. Map what is actually happening in 2026 vs sci-fi.
3. The investable question is: **where does the gap between capability and diffusion create Monastery-stage companies** (seed–A, $2M, 5%, 12 weeks of leverage)?
4. Consumer/education/entertainment apps can look like wrappers. Demand a real answer on moat vs Big Tech.
