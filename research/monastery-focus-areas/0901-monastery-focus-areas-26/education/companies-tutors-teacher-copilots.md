## Teacher Copilots & Classroom AI

**Khan Academy / Khanmigo** (khanacademy.org, US, founded 2008, nonprofit). Free AI tutor (Khanmigo for students) and teaching assistant (Khanmigo for Teachers), built on OpenAI's GPT models with Google Gemini now layered in for interactive visual tutoring, funded by philanthropy rather than VC. Buyer: donors (Microsoft funds free US-teacher access) and school districts (paid Khan Academy Districts tier). Differentiation: nonprofit trust + Sal Khan brand + free access model that VC-backed competitors can't match on price; largest scale claim in the sector (2.0M signups SY24-25, +731% YoY). Traction: 770K US district students on integrated Khanmigo; new Gemini-powered features shipped for the 2026 school year via a 6-month Google.org engineering fellowship. Status: Alive, accelerating. Sources: [Khan Academy Annual Report SY24-25](https://annualreport.khanacademy.org/), 2026; [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/khan-academy-back-to-school/), 2026; [EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/khan-academy-brings-gemini-powered-visual-tutoring-into-classrooms-for-2026), 2026.

**MagicSchool AI** (magicschool.ai, US, founded 2023, Series B, ~$63M raised). AI toolkit for teachers — lesson planning, grading assistance, IEP drafting, communications — sold via freemium teacher signup that upsells to district enterprise contracts. Buyer: individual teachers (free) → district administrators (paid enterprise, funded via Title I/II/III/IV and state allocations). Differentiation: claims ~8M educator signups across 160 countries and "1 in 5 US schoolchildren" in a partner school — the largest teacher-copilot distribution claim in the category; founder Adeel Khan is an ex-teacher/assistant principal, giving credibility with buyers. Traction: 10,000+ schools, 3x YoY revenue growth (as of last fiscal year); named district customers include Denver PS, Broward County, Atlanta PS. Risk flagged by outside analysts: unclear what share of the 8M signups are paying vs. free, and district budgets are annual/slow to convert. Status: Alive, scaling. Sources: [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/educator-built-edtech-startup-ai-magicschool-kahn/); [MagicSchool districts release](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/magicschool-names-9-u-school-151600220.html), Jun 10 2026; [Value Add Pulse](https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/magicschool-ai-edtech-63m-funding-2026), 2026.

**SchoolAI** (schoolai.com, US/Lehi UT, founded 2021/2023, Series A, $32M total). All-in-one AI platform for K-12 classroom instruction with built-in teacher monitoring/controls, 1:1 tutoring "Spaces," and a "Mission Control" teacher dashboard. Buyer: schools/districts, with a free-tier teacher-led motion. Differentiation: monitoring/guardrails-first design pitched at superintendents worried about unsupervised student-AI chat, vs. more student-facing-only tools. Traction: "used in 1M+ classrooms across 80+ countries" at time of Series A; 144 employees (+58% YoY). $25M Series A closed April 2025 led by Insight Partners; no Series B found as of Sept 2026 (third-party trackers estimate an ~$84M valuation, unverified). Status: Alive. Sources: [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/04/02/3054126/0/en/SchoolAI-Secures-25-Million-to-Help-Teachers-and-Schools-Reach-Every-Student.html), Apr 2025; [LinkedIn company data](https://linkedin.com/company/getschoolai).

**Brisk Teaching** (briskteaching.com, US/San Francisco, founded 2024, Series A, $21.9M total). Browser-extension-based AI agent for K-12 teachers — lesson creation from any webpage, grading/feedback, and AI-writing-detection tools for academic integrity. Buyer: individual teachers (PLG, 90%+ inbound) converting to school/district licenses. Differentiation: browser-extension form factor (works inside whatever tool the teacher already has open) plus a specific AI-detection/authenticity angle that resonates with teacher anxiety about student AI misuse. Traction: 1M+ educators in 100+ countries, 2,000+ partner schools, reported "40x'd revenue in 2024," 1-in-5 US K-12 teachers had installed the extension as of Feb 2025. $15M Series A (Mar 2025, Bessemer) on top of $5M seed (Sep 2024) — no new round found through Sept 2026. Status: Alive. Sources: [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brisk-teaching-raises-15-million-to-reinvent-classroom-technology-for-the-ai-era-302412034.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/ais-coming-to-the-classroom-brisk-raises-15m-after-a-quick-start-in-school/), Mar 2025.

**Curipod** (curipod.com, Norway/US, founded 2019, seed, $4.6M total). AI lesson-generator that turns a topic prompt into an interactive slideshow/discussion activity for K-12 classrooms. Buyer: individual teachers, freemium. Differentiation: narrower, simpler scope than MagicSchool/Brisk (one job: generate an interactive lesson fast) rather than an all-in-one suite. Traction: 150,000+ teachers signed up across all 50 US states plus Norway at time of its 2023 seed. No public funding or traction update found for 2025-26 — may be quietly profitable/small, or losing share to broader suites. Status: Alive but quiet; recommend a fresh check before weighting heavily in a memo. Sources: [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/pitch-deck-edtech-startup-curipod-raise-seed-funding-2023-9), 2023; [CBInsights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/curipod).

**TeachFX** (teachfx.com, US, founded 2016, seed/A, ~$10M+ total). Voice-AI classroom-discourse analytics — records and analyzes teacher talk time, question types, wait time, and student "uptake" to give teachers feedback on pedagogy, not just content generation. Buyer: districts (instructional-coaching budget line, not just a teacher tool). Differentiation: the only company in this list whose core product is analyzing *teaching quality* via voice AI rather than generating content — closer to a "Gong.io for classrooms" than a lesson-plan generator; also has a Gates Foundation grant to extend the tech into online tutoring contexts. Traction: 8,500+ teachers across all 50 states, reported 9x YoY growth, 100%+ license utilization in partner districts. Status: Alive. Sources: [TeachFX blog](https://teachfx.com/blog/letter-from-the-founderteachfx-raises-10m-to-create-more-meaningful-and-equitable-classroom-discourse).

**Diffit** (diffit.me, US, founded 2023, seed, undisclosed small amount). Generates differentiated/leveled versions of any text or topic for mixed-ability classrooms. Buyer: individual teachers. Differentiation: narrow, single-purpose tool (differentiation/reading-level adaptation specifically) rather than a broad suite — a feature that MagicSchool/SchoolAI/Brisk also all offer natively, which is Diffit's biggest competitive threat (platform risk). Traction: not disclosed publicly; one seed round (Jan 2024, Hyde Park Venture Partners + Workshop Venture Partners) is the only funding event found. Status: Alive, small. Sources: [CBInsights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/diffit/financials).

**Superhuman (formerly Grammarly)** (superhuman.com, US, founded 2009, late-stage/PE-scale). Renamed from Grammarly to Superhuman in Nov 2025, unifying Grammarly's writing assistant, Coda, and Superhuman Mail under an agentic AI-productivity umbrella; the education product is "Superhuman Go for Education," an always-on AI assistant embedded across a student/institution's existing tools (Docs, Canvas, Gmail), plus "Authorship," a tool that gives teachers transparent, agent-level attribution of AI use in student writing. Buyer: higher-ed institutions (first flagship partner: Arizona State University) and K-12/university writing-center budgets historically served by Grammarly for Education. Differentiation: incumbent distribution (3,000+ educational institution customers, 40M+ users pre-rebrand) plus a specific academic-integrity attribution feature most pure-play copilots lack. Traction: ASU is the first Superhuman Go higher-ed deployment; broader K-12/higher-ed rollout still early. Status: Alive, repositioning company-wide, education is one vertical among several. Sources: [Grammarly/Superhuman blog](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/company/announcing-company-rebrand-to-superhuman/), Nov 2025; [EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/grammarly-announces-first-superhuman-go-partnership-in-higher-education-at-arizona-state-university).

**Microsoft Copilot for Education** (microsoft.com/education, US, Big Tech business unit). Copilot integrated into Microsoft 365 Education (Word, Teams, OneNote) plus Microsoft's earlier free-Khanmigo-for-teachers funding partnership with Khan Academy. Buyer: school IT/district enterprise agreements bundled with existing Microsoft 365 licenses. Differentiation: bundling/distribution advantage into schools already on Microsoft 365 rather than a standalone purchase decision — a structural Big Tech moat against point-solution startups. Status: Alive, no separate funding/financials disclosed as a business line. Sources: general Microsoft Education product pages; [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/21/microsoft-khan-academy-launch-free-ai-assistant-for-all-us-teachers.html) (re: Khanmigo funding partnership).

**Google Gemini for Education (+ NotebookLM)** (edu.google.com, US, Big Tech business unit). Gemini 2.5 Pro-based assistant built into Google Workspace for Education, plus NotebookLM (source-grounded research/study notebook), Gemini Live, and "Gems" custom agents, with under-18 safety guardrails. Buyer: school districts already on Google Workspace for Education (included free in the Workspace edition) or via standalone "Google for Education Fundamentals" signup. Differentiation: zero-incremental-cost bundling into the dominant K-12 productivity suite (Google Workspace has the largest US K-12 seat share) — the single biggest distribution threat to independent teacher-copilot and student-study-tool startups. Traction: reinstated free 12-month "Google AI Pro for students" offer (US) / free AI Plus (140+ other markets) from Aug 19, 2026 after a brief lapse (student offer had expired Mar 11, 2026). Status: Alive, aggressively expanding. Sources: [Google for Education](https://edu.google.com/ai/gemini-for-education/); [Glasp comparison](https://glasp.co/articles/ai-study-modes-compared), 2026.

**OpenAI — ChatGPT Study Mode / ChatGPT Edu** (openai.com, US, product launched Jul 29 2025). A dedicated "Study Mode" surface inside ChatGPT (all plans, including Free) that enforces Socratic hint-based tutoring instead of direct answers, alongside the separate institutional "ChatGPT Edu" enterprise offering. Buyer: individual students (free) and universities (ChatGPT Edu licenses). Differentiation: largest existing consumer AI user base of any player in this list — Study Mode converts existing ChatGPT habit into a "more honest" tutoring interaction rather than requiring a new app download; positioned by reviewers as best for "fast review, high-volume practice, voice-based drills" versus Claude's edge on deep conceptual work. Status: Alive. Sources: [Glasp](https://glasp.co/articles/claude-vs-chatgpt-for-learning), 2026; [buildmvpfast.com](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/articles/best-llms-2026-guide/education-ai), Jul 2026.

**Anthropic — Claude for Education** (claude.com/education, US, product launched 2025). "Learning mode" toggle for individuals (free) plus a full institutional Claude for Education product with Canvas/Wiley/Panopto integrations and large context windows for universities. Buyer: individual students (free tier) and universities (institutional licensing, e.g., campus-wide agreements). Differentiation: positioned by independent reviewers as strongest for "deep conceptual work, dense reading, humanities essays" — deliberately slower/more Socratic than ChatGPT, betting on pedagogy quality over speed/convenience. Status: Alive. Sources: [Glasp](https://glasp.co/articles/ai-study-modes-compared), 2026; [vecosys.com](https://www.vecosys.com/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-gemini-school-workflow/), 2026.

**Perplexity for Education** (perplexity.ai, US, product launched ~2024). Student-focused Perplexity access (campus/student Pro programs) leaning on Perplexity's core search-with-citations product rather than a purpose-built tutoring UX. Buyer: individual students. Differentiation: citation-grounded answers are useful for research/essay-sourcing tasks specifically, but Perplexity has meaningfully less 2026 trade-press coverage in the "AI study tool" comparison set than ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini — a smaller mindshare position in this specific vertical despite Perplexity's broader consumer traction. Status: Alive, but a minor player in the education-specific comparison set. Sources: general Perplexity product pages; comparative absence in [buildmvpfast.com](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/articles/best-llms-2026-guide/education-ai) top-10 list is itself a data point.

## AI Tutors (Adaptive Learning / Conversational)

**Synthesis Tutor** (synthesis.com, US, founded 2020, Series B-stage but financially stressed, $17.5-53M raised depending on source). AI-powered 1:1 math/reasoning tutoring app for children ages ~5-11, grown out of Josh Dahn's SpaceX-era "Ad Astra" experimental school. Buyer: parents (direct-to-consumer subscription) plus a "Synthesis Teams" collaborative-problem-solving product for schools. Differentiation: pedagogy rooted in complex-problem-solving/simulation-based learning (not drill-and-practice) and a notable founder story (Elon Musk-commissioned school), rather than curriculum-alignment or district-procurement muscle. Traction: SEC Reg CF filings show revenue growing modestly (+7% YoY to $10.98M in FY2025) with narrowing losses, but flag a real risk: a **$10.6M convertible note matured June 15, 2025 and remained unresolved/outstanding** as of the FY2025 annual report, with the company citing 6-12 months of cash runway. Status: Alive but under real balance-sheet stress — a useful cautionary data point on capital efficiency in consumer AI-tutor subscription businesses. Sources: [SEC Reg CF Annual Report FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1857145/000185714526000005/C-AR_Annual_Report-FY-2025.pdf); [NeuronFeed](https://neuronfeed.com/startups/synthesis), 2026.

**Third Space Learning** (thirdspacelearning.com, UK, founded 2013, growth-stage, £30M+ cumulative). Spoken (voice) AI math tutor "Skye," built on a decade of the company's own human 1:1-tutoring dialogue, pacing, and assessment data, delivered to primary/secondary students via schools. Buyer: UK/US schools and districts, plus a Gates Foundation-funded free-to-district program (3,000 US students, 2026/27 school year). Differentiation: the only company in this catalog running a rigorous, published research partnership (Stanford SCALE + Cornell National Tutoring Observatory, $1.9M Gates grant) specifically to measure AI-tutoring efficacy against the evidence base for high-impact human tutoring — most competitors lead with usage/engagement claims, not learning-outcome studies. Traction: 196,000+ students across 4,200+ schools historically; £4.4M growth round closed April 2026 to fund US expansion. Status: Alive, growing, and pushing into the US. Sources: [Third Space Learning press release](https://thirdspacelearning.com/press/investment-april-2026/), Apr 2026; [pe-deals.com](https://pe-deals.com/deal/third-space-learning-210d8a30-da12-4258-917c-1afd668d7eb8).

**Kyron Learning** (kyronlearning.com, US, founded 2022, Series A, $15.45M total). AI-mediated "interactive video" tutoring — two-way branching conversations layered onto pre-recorded real-instructor video, plus "Kyron Studio" for auto-generating new interactive courses. Buyer: K-12 schools and higher-ed (piloted with Western Governors University, Illinois Institute of Technology). Differentiation: video-based instructor-presence model (a real teacher's face/voice, branching via AI) vs. pure text/voice chatbot tutors — targets the "1/10th the cost of a live tutor" positioning with a founder team (ex-Google Cloud AI VP CEO, Qwiklabs founder CTO) credible on the infrastructure side. Traction: 35+ pilot schools for K-12 math; revenue figures are inconsistent across sources ($300K-$6.1M) and should be verified directly. No new funding round found since the Dec 2023 Series A. Status: Alive, early-scale. Sources: [Kyron Learning LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/kyronlearning); [Faculty Focus](https://www.facultyfocus.com/articles/teaching-with-technology-articles/designing-the-2026-classroom-emerging-learning-trends-in-an-ai-powered-education-system/), 2026.

**Squirrel AI Learning** (squirrelai.com, China/Shanghai, founded 2014, Series C/unicorn, $194-295M raised depending on source). AI-adaptive K-12 tutoring system (the "IALS" — Intelligent Adaptive Learning System) originally delivered via after-school tutoring centers plus smart hardware (tablets/pens). Buyer: originally parents via tutoring centers; post-2021 China crackdown, increasingly B2B licensing and hardware sales, plus a planned separate US market entry in 2026 via an independent US tech platform to sidestep China-regulatory/geopolitical risk. Differentiation: one of the earliest (pre-LLM) large-scale AI-adaptive-learning deployments globally, with a decade of granular learning-data collection underpinning its "multimodal" personalization claims. Differentiation: the deepest historical capital base and largest pre-crackdown scale of any China-education company still operating (post-BYJU'S-style implosion risk avoided so far by aggressive diversification), with 2,400+ A100-equivalent GPUs for real-time AI-tutoring infrastructure. Traction: FY2025 revenue RMB 8.2B (~$1.1B); hardware now ~35% of group revenue, B2B AI licensing RMB 1.9B (~$270M, ~18% of revenue) — i.e., "pure tutoring" is now a minority of the business. Status: Alive, successfully diversified/pivoted rather than pure survival. Sources: [AskCyborg](https://askcyborg.com/preview/yuanfudao), 2026; [Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2020-10-23/online-educator-yuanfudao-raises-22-billion-as-valuation-doubles-101617767.html), 2020 (funding history).

Baidu spinoff turned homework-help/live-tutoring platform, backed by Alibaba, HongShan Capital, and SoftBank Vision Fund; also parent of the global Question.AI homework-scanning app (see above). Buyer: Chinese K-12 families (domestic) and global consumers (via Question.AI). Differentiation: the Question.AI global consumer product gives Zuoyebang a second growth vector outside the constrained domestic China tutoring market that Yuanfudao and Squirrel AI lack at the same scale. Traction/status: **confidentially filed for a US IPO** (Bloomberg reporting, 2026), targeting a raise of possibly under $100M — a dramatically scaled-down ambition versus its pre-crackdown 2021 plan to raise $500M+ at a $10B valuation, signaling the market still prices China-education-exposed companies at a steep discount even years after the regulatory crackdown eased. Status: Alive, IPO-track but not yet public. Sources: [NDA News/Bloomberg](https://au.nda.news/initial-public-offering-st_36104/news/zuoyebang-said-to-file-for-us-ipo-as-china-edtech-campaign-wanes-sn_5787220/), 2026.

## India / Global Tutoring — Consolidation & Casualties

**Physics Wallah** (pw.live, India, founded 2016, public NSE: PHYS, IPO Nov 2025). Test-prep and K-12/upskilling platform that grew from founder Alakh Pandey's YouTube channel, now India's first profitable-trending post-crackdown edtech IPO success story. Buyer: Indian students/families (online + 300+ offline test-prep centers) for competitive-exam prep (JEE/NEET-style) and increasingly K-12 ("Curious Junior") and state-board content. Differentiation: explicitly pursuing an AI-first cost-reduction strategy — building in-house small language models trained on Indian STEM data and its own proprietary student-engagement dataset, which management claims can match or beat frontier-model accuracy at a fraction of the cost for its specific curriculum use case, rather than paying full frontier-model API prices at its ARPU levels (~₹4,000/year, ~₹10/day). Traction: IPO'd Nov 18 2025 at ~$3.6B, closed day one up 44-49% to a ~$5-5.2B market cap; FY26 revenue +35% YoY to ₹3,900 Cr (~$460M) on a 74% 3-year CAGR; AI Tutor pilot showed 95% lesson-level accuracy, <1% hallucination rate, $0.20/hour pilot cost across 300+ students; explicitly pausing K-12 offline campus capex to go "100% asset-light" and AI-first instead. Status: Alive, profitable-trending, the clearest "AI-native pivot from a legacy content business" success case in this entire catalog. Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/18/physics-wallah-enjoys-a-rosy-ipo-day-bucking-the-broader-slowdown-in-indian-edtech/), Nov 2025; [FY26 shareholder letter](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/PhysicsWallah-Q4-shareholder-letter.pdf), May 2026.

**Vedantu** (vedantu.com, India, founded 2011, Series F, $332-337M total raised). Live-interactive K-12 tutoring platform for competitive exams (JEE/NEET/Olympiads) and general schooling. Buyer: Indian families, direct-to-consumer subscription. Differentiation: one of the few India live-tutoring unicorns to survive the BYJU'S-triggered sector collapse, but its most recent raise (**just $11M in Sept 2025**, vs. a $100M Series E in 2021) signals meaningful capital scarcity relative to its earlier growth-stage ambitions. Status: Alive, but capital-constrained relative to history; a useful data point on how much smaller "success" now looks in India edtech post-2022. Sources: [Inc42 funding tracker](https://inc42.com/company/vedantu/funding/); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/vedantu/__jrLUroips5-2o0-xzJxe9tR17nhmFFeccl9XfQ3CuLA).

**BYJU'S** (byjus.com, India, founded 2011, insolvency proceedings). Once India's most valuable startup and the largest edtech company globally by valuation ($22B peak, 2022) — content, live-tutoring, and hardware (tablets) bundled into aggressive door-to-door/EMI-financed sales. Buyer: was Indian families via aggressive consumer sales; now effectively no active buyer as the company is in insolvency. Status: **Dead** — filed for insolvency July 16, 2024 on a ₹158-crore BCCI claim; lenders (GLAS Trust, representing a $1.2B term loan) hold >99% of creditor voting power; India's NCLT Bengaluru **stayed the Form G creditor-bidding/resolution process until Aug 31, 2026** pending a founder challenge to the claim quantum, and the resolution professional sought a further 90-day extension past the original 180-day window that expired July 29, 2026. A stark illustration of terminal asset illiquidity in hardware+content edtech: ~200,000 unsold tablets have sat in warehouses since March 2026, with warehouse storage fees alone running ₹20-30 lakh/month. This is the single most important negative case study for any thesis considering hardware-bundled or aggressive-consumer-financing edtech models. Sources: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/nclt-pauses-byju-s-insolvency-bidding-till-aug-31-giving-founders-relief-126072301153_1.html), Jul 2026; [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/byjus-tablets-laptops-gather-dust-in-warehouse-as-insolvency-drags-on/articleshow/132114641.cms).

**Unacademy** (unacademy.com, India, founded 2015, acquired by upGrad in an all-stock deal, closed Sept 1 2026). Test-prep/creator-led learning platform (PrepLadder medical-exam-prep, Graphy creator-monetization tools, Airlearn language-learning app). Buyer at exit: upGrad (a higher-ed/upskilling platform), all-stock transaction. Status: **Alive but absorbed / effectively a down-exit** — sold for ~$206M, roughly **94% below** its 2021 peak valuation of $3.44B, even though the underlying business had ₹900 crore (~$100M+) cash on hand and most business lines were profitable or near-profitable at the time of sale; founder Gaurav Munjal continues as CEO of the Unacademy Group within upGrad. A second major India-edtech consolidation event in the same week as this catalog's compilation date, alongside the ongoing BYJU'S insolvency saga — together they illustrate that even India-edtech survivors with real cash and real profitability are choosing consolidation over continuing to raise venture capital independently. Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/indias-unacademy-sells-to-rival-upgrad-for-206m-about-94-less-than-its-peak-valuation/), Sept 1 2026; [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/upgrad-completes-all-stock-acquisition-unacademy-group-usd-200-million-deal-19981650.htm).

## Data-Quality Flags (read before using in an IC memo)

1. **Riiid name collision** — the education-focused Riiid Inc. (Seoul, founded 2014, Santa TOEIC/SAT test-prep, SoftBank-backed) is a **different company** from a newer (2021-founded) Korean "Riiid"-branded AI-portal/consumer-companion-chat business that hit a KRW 1 trillion+ ("fifth Korean AI unicorn") valuation in August 2026. Do not cite the August 2026 unicorn news as evidence of the education company's traction.
2. **Lower-confidence entries not independently re-verified this session with a dedicated fresh search**: Quizlet, Kahoot! (ownership status), Tutor.com, GoStudent, Cognii, Area9 Lyceum, Knowbox, Rori, Knowji, Mindgrasp. General-knowledge-sourced; recommend a targeted follow-up search on each before using specific numbers in an investment memo.
3. **Funding totals vary 10-30% across aggregators** (Crunchbase, Tracxn, Pitchbook, CBInsights, LinkedIn-derived figures) for nearly every private company in this catalog. Where a primary source (SEC filing, company press release, direct investor announcement) was available, it is cited and preferred; where only aggregator data existed, a range is given and flagged.
4. **Third-party valuation-tracker sites** (PremierAlts, Notice.co, Caplight, Indexed.vc, TechStackIPO) produce modeled/secondary-market valuation estimates, not primary-round data. These are used only where no primary source existed and are labeled "unverified" or "estimate" inline.
5. **The single most important structural pattern across this catalog**: (a) US/UK teacher-copilot startups (MagicSchool, SchoolAI, Brisk) are raising modest ($15-25M) Series A rounds against huge usage-signup numbers but thin disclosed revenue/conversion data; (b) the "AI homework helper" category (Chegg, Course Hero, Socratic) is being actively destroyed by free frontier LLMs and Google AI Overviews; (c) China and India tutoring giants (Yuanfudao, Zuoyebang, Squirrel AI, BYJU'S, Unacademy) were all forced into hardware/B2B pivots, insolvency, or consolidation by regulatory or capital-market shocks, a cautionary parallel for any US/EU consumer-tutoring-app thesis that assumes venture-scale growth is durable; and (d) the biggest disclosed dollar outcomes in "AI + learning" this cycle are Big Tech's own bundled products (Google Gemini for Education, Microsoft Copilot for Education, OpenAI/Anthropic study modes, all free-or-near-free) and one large enterprise-L&D acquisition (Sana Labs → Workday, ~$1.1B) — not a venture-backed K-12/consumer-tutoring exit.
