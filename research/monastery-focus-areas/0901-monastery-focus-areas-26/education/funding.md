## 1. Market context: funding is down, concentration is up

- **H1 2026 global EdTech VC: ~$1.0B, down 26% YoY** from $1.35B in H1 2025. Deal count held flat — average deal size is what shrank (mega-rounds vanished). East Asia (+37% deal volume) and **Early Childhood Education (deal count more than tripled YoY)** are the two bright spots. — HolonIQ, Jul 2026 ([source](https://www.holoniq.com/notes/1b-in-edtech-venture-capital-to-date-funding-falls-short-of-last-years-midpoint-asia-mena-buck-the-trend))
- **Multi-year arc:** 2020 $16.1B → 2021 $20.8B (peak) → 2022 $10.6B → 2023 $3.0B → 2024 $2.3B → 2025 $2.6B (+11%, "stabilizing") → 2026 not continuing the recovery YoY. — HolonIQ 2026 Global Education Outlook
- **AI is now most of the category:** AI-focused education startups raised **$4.2B in 2025 = 62% of all edtech VC funding**; AI tutors + educator-copilots alone = ~85% of that $4.2B. >2,800 "AI education" startups exist now vs. ~155 in 2023 (18x). — ValueAdd VC / HolonIQ synthesis, 2026 ([source](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/ai-tutors-are-here-how-duolingo-khan-academy-and-startups-are-personalizing-education))
- **Sober mood at ASU+GSV 2026** (the industry's flagship event, San Diego): "cap tables feel tight," several dedicated edtech VCs haven't returned promised LPs, no one expects a healthy M&A market or IPO window in the next 12 months; capital rotating toward "workflow-embedded" and outcomes-evidenced models over pure B2C subscription bets. — Edtech Insiders / Tyton Partners recaps ([1](https://edtechinsiders.substack.com/p/the-edtech-insiders-rundown-of-asugsv-55b), [2](https://tytonpartners.com/asugsv-2026-what-we-heard-in-our-conversations-and-between-the-lines/))
- **The sector's central bottleneck data point:** Khanmigo (Khan Academy, free via Microsoft since May 2024) grew signups 731% YoY to ~700K active users — but **only ~15% of students with access use it regularly**. Free distribution has decoupled from engagement, and it's becoming the standard skeptic's objection at every pitch. — Khan Academy SY24-25 Annual Report; ValueAdd VC, 2026

## 4. Failures / downrounds

**BYJU'S (Think & Learn)** — peak **$22B** (Oct 2022) → BlackRock mark-downs to ~$11B (Apr 2023) → ~$1B (early 2024) → insolvency. Built on debt-fueled M&A (Aakash ~$950M–$3B, Epic! $500M, WhiteHat Jr $300M, etc.) funded partly by a **$1.2B term loan (Nov 2021)** that became the trigger when audited financials came in 17 months late showing ₹4,588cr losses. US unit filed Chapter 11 Feb 2024; Indian parent entered NCLT insolvency Jul 2024. **As of Sep 1, 2026, still unresolved** — NCLT has repeatedly paused the bidder-invitation process over a founder challenge to lenders' claim size; practical valuation now ~$0–500M. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/03/01/the-rise-and-fall-of-byjus-once-a-startup-darling-in-india.html), [NCLT coverage Aug 2026](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/in-relief-to-founders-nclt-stays-byjus-form-g-bidding-process-till-next-hearing/article71260995.ece))

**2U / edX** — Chapter 11 filed Jul 25, 2024 (~$945M debt, mostly from the **$800M 2021 edX acquisition** made on a pandemic-demand bet that didn't hold). Restructuring halved debt to ~$459M, converted lenders to equity holders, emerged private Sep 13, 2024. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-07-25/2u-owner-of-edx-online-courses-files-chapter-11-bankruptcy), [2U press release](https://2u.com/newsroom/2u-successfully-completes-transaction/))

**Chegg** — see public comps: **-99% market cap** from Dec-2020 peak ($11.64B) to ~$91–95M (Sep 2026); management and press directly attribute this to free ChatGPT-class tools substituting for paid homework help.

## 5. Typical seed–A valuations

**Consumer AI tutor / language / subject apps (B2C or B2B2C-via-parent):**
- Pre-seed: $1–8M raised, ~$10–25M implied post (Sparkli, Wippi, Giant, Wild Zebra).
- Seed: $2–8M checks, $8–25M post typical (Medly AI, Class Companion).
- Series A: $15–25M raised, **$80–450M post** — wide range because pricing follows engagement/DAU trajectory, not ARR (SchoolAI $84M, Brisk $83.5M, MagicSchool $100M→$431M in 7 months).
- Breakout case: **Speak reached $1B in ~30 months**, doubling from a $500M Series B extension (Jun 2024) to $1B Series C (Dec 2024) on $100M+ ARR.

**B2B school/district SaaS:**
- Seed can be primed surprisingly low even with brand-name edtech VCs: MagicSchool's Aug-2023 Series A-4 priced at just **$13.89M post** off $2.42M, backed by Reach Capital, GSV Ventures, Rethink Education, Charter School Growth Fund.
- Series A clusters $15–46M raised at $80–100M+ post *once there's a credible district pilot base* (SchoolAI, Brisk, Campus).
- Growth is gated by district budget cycles (6–12 months, RFP-heavy), not virality — valuations expand more slowly per funding stage than consumer apps.

**Monastery read:** a $2M check (via uncapped SAFE) at an implied ~$40M post-equivalent is aggressive for pure pre-seed in this sector but squarely inside the "hot seed / Series-A-adjacent" band once there's *some* usage or pilot signal — reasonable for consumer AI-tutor teams with early retention data, too small to anchor a district-validated school-SaaS Series A on its own, and too rich for a bare idea with no users.

## 6. Unit economics — parent LTV vs. district ACV

| | B2C (parent-paid) | B2B (district) |
|---|---|---|
| Typical ACV | **$50–500/yr** per household | **$4K (small charter) – $4M (Tier-1 25K+ student district)** |
| LTV:CAC benchmark | 2:1 – 4:1 | 3:1 – 6:1 |
| CAC payback | 6–18 months | 12–24 months |
| Sales cycle | instant (app store) | 6–12 months, RFP-heavy |
| Paid conversion | Duolingo ~9% (paid subs / MAU) is the high-water mark; K-12 freemium-to-paid runs 3–7% generally | N/A — logo-based, multi-year contracts (3–5 yr typical, 15–25% multi-year prepay discounts) |
| PSPY (per-student-per-year) reference | — | $6–14 supplemental (i-Ready/IXL-class); $12–28 core curriculum; $35–80 SIS/LMS; $95–180 assessment/analytics |
| Structural risk | Big Tech giving away free comparable tools (Google Gemini "Guided Learning," Aug 2025; free OpenAI/Anthropic study tools) | **ESSER/E-Rate funding cliff** — $190B ARPA ESSER pool fully obligated by Sep 2024; post-stimulus district budgets are the real test of 2025–2026 vintage school-SaaS ARR |
| Concrete data points | Praktika: ~$20M TTM revenue / 1.2M MAU at Series A (May 2024); Speak: $100M+ ARR (2025), first AI-language product past that line | MagicSchool: "6M educators, thousands of districts" cited at Series B (Feb 2025) as the traction proof for a $431M post |

**Net:** parent LTV is small per-user but scales instantly and globally; district ACV is large per logo but slow and budget-cycle-fragile. A $2M Monastery check buys ~12–24 months of runway either way, but a B2C team needs real paid-conversion data (not downloads) within that window, while a B2B team needs at least one signed district or state pilot to justify a follow-on Series A.

## 8. Monastery lens

**Deal math:** $2M (uncapped SAFE) + 5% equity ≈ a **~$40M post-money reference point** if read as priced equity (2M / 0.05), though the actual uncapped-SAFE-plus-separate-equity-grant structure means Monastery isn't fixing that price — it's buying 5% outright plus extending bridge capital that reprices at the company's next round.

**Fit assessment vs. comps:**
- **Too big for bare pre-seed/idea-stage** — Sparkli ($5M), Wippi ($1.2M), Giant ($8M) show whole seed rounds for pre-traction consumer-AI-education teams close in the $1–8M range from a syndicate, not a single $2M check. A Monastery check would plausibly *be* the entire seed round here — appropriate for Monastery's model.
- **Reasonable as a syndicate component at "hot seed"** — comparable to a slice of MagicSchool's ($15.3M/$100M post) or SchoolAI's ($25M/$84M post) Series A.
- **Too small to anchor a district-validated school-SaaS Series A** ($15–46M rounds) — best used as bridge/pre-seed capital ahead of a "real" Series A from Owl/GSV/Reach.
- **Best-fit profiles for a $2M Monastery check:** (1) consumer AI-tutor/language/subject apps, 2–10 person teams, pre-Series-A with early retention data — matches the Medly AI / Wild Zebra / Class Companion stage; (2) early-childhood/parenting/child-development AI products — the one subsegment HolonIQ shows *tripling* in deal count YoY, with a thinner/less-specialized investor base (12 Flags, Founderful) where Monastery's SF AI-native network is genuinely additive.
- **Poor fit:** district-facing school SaaS pre-pilot (6–12 month sales cycles don't compress in a 12-week sprint); anything already Series-B+ in financial distress (BYJU'S/Chegg/2U-style — wrong stage, wrong problem for a product-velocity accelerator).
