## Original strategy under test

AI-powered vertical roll-up in **call center operations** or adjacent verticals:

1. Acquire or build specialized call centers in specific verticals (telecom, e-commerce, debt collection)
2. Hybrid AI-human ops (AI on standard flows, humans on complex)
3. Scale via tech licensing or operational excellence
4. Exit via strategic acquisition or continued scaling

## Original research dimensions

Market opportunity (TAM US/EU/global; vertical willingness to pay; cost structures; % cost reduction); competitive landscape (Observe.AI, Cresta, PolyAI, Sierra, Parloa, Replicant, Gridspace); verticals (telecom, e-commerce, debt collection, home services); moats (data, compliance PCI/HIPAA/GDPR, integration); GTM; exit (Five9, Genesys, NICE, Talkdesk, Teleperformance, Concentrix, TTEC, Salesforce, Microsoft, Zendesk, ServiceNow, PE); failure modes and commoditization.
