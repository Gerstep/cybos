# AI Roll-Ups & AI-Native Service Firms — Company Catalog

**Date**: 2026-09-01 · **Updates**: `prior-research-2025-10.md` (call-center-only thesis) · **Companion**: `../raw/agent-company-researcher-rollups.md` (narrative + full source trail)

**Methodology note**: Exa/Perplexity/Parallel MCP tools were not available in this cloud-agent environment; research used `WebSearch`/`WebFetch` against ~30 queries and ~60 sources (news, company blogs, Sacra/Dealroom/Tracxn/CB Insights teardowns, PitchBook). All claims below are dated and sourced. Company or investor press releases are the primary source for most disclosed metrics — treat operating numbers as directional, not audited.

**Legend — Model column**:
- **Buy→AI**: acquires existing services businesses, then layers AI onto the acquired P&L (the General Catalyst / Thrive Holdings playbook)
- **AI→Buy / Organic**: built as an AI-native operating service from inception; may still make bolt-on acquisitions, but the core is delivering the service, not licensing software
- **SaaS-into-vertical** 🚩: sells software/subscriptions to incumbent operators; does **not** own the service P&L. Flagged per brief — these are NOT Monastery-relevant unless there's an explicit wedge-to-ownership signal (noted where present).
- **Incumbent+AI vendor** 🏛️: large legacy operator bolting AI onto an existing business, or a corporate/PE consolidation without an AI-native design claim — "too-late comp," not a Monastery-stage opportunity.

**Company count: 54** (target range 40–70; see gaps/negative findings at bottom).

## Cross-Cutting Findings for the GP

1. **Buy→AI is capital-heavy by design.** GC states $100–150M per portfolio company across 3–4 rounds; Thrive Holdings just raised $2B at a $12B valuation; Beacon raised >$550M in <2 years. None of this is $2M/12-week shaped. If Monastery wants exposure to this pattern, it is via seeding the AI→Buy/Organic model (Crosby, Tenex-style, Comeryx-style) at true inception, before the first big round — or by treating buy→AI holdcos purely as strategic/exit context, not as portfolio targets.
2. **CX/call-center is confirmed too late**, exactly as the brief anticipated: Sierra ($15.8B), Decagon ($4.5B), and GC's own Crescendo already occupy this space; Concentrix/Teleperformance are the incumbent casualties. No fresh $2M wedge is visible here.

4. **Dental DSO looks structurally blocked** for the buy→AI model by corporate-practice-of-dentistry law in many US states — a useful negative case showing not every "PE loves this vertical" market is open to the GC/Thrive playbook.
5. **The SaaS→services wedge is real but nascent**: Adaptive/LLŪM, EvenUp's pre-litigation-funding move, and Klarity's rebrand to "Within"/Company Brain are the clearest signals of vertical-SaaS companies edging toward owning delivery. Worth a dedicated follow-up pass in ~1 quarter.
