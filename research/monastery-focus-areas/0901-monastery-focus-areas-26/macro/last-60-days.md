## What actually changes the 5-year map (top 5, ranked)

1. **Agent tooling crossed a real capability threshold (Aug 19).** Anthropic's Browser Use + Computer Use GA gives agents structured, accessibility-tree-based control of arbitrary web software with no API — the missing piece for automating the back-office SaaS workflows every services roll-up needs at 30-70% automation to hit its margin thesis. This is more actionable for Monastery than any single roll-up deal this window: it's a building block a $2M team can ship on top of *today*.

2. **The roll-up lane has left seed-stage reach.** Thrive at $12B (new vertical: physical infrastructure) + Long Lake/GC taking a >$3B public company (Amex GBT) private confirms this is now institutional-scale capital only, moving into *more* regulated verticals precisely because digital services are crowded. Monastery's roll-up angle, if any, is selling picks-and-shovels into this ecosystem, not building a roll-up.

3. **Education's free-distribution lane is closing; the structured-outcome lane is open and fundable at exactly Monastery scale.** OpenAI/Anthropic giving away chatbots to 300K+ teachers kills "wrapper for teachers" as a business. Medly ($8M seed, government contract, attainment study) and Wild Zebra ($6M seed, explicitly pitched against the free labs) show the fundable pattern: curriculum-specific, outcome-measured, government-procurement-savvy.

4. **Generative-Netflix tech readiness is 12-24 months out, not now.** Every serious 2026 real-time world-model system (Magpie, Matrix-Game 3.5, AlayaWorld, Project Genie) still separates deterministic logic from generative visuals and caps consistency at minutes. The fundable layer today is tools for human-directed generation (Higgsfield $700M ARR, Osmo, Flova) — the "new studio type" bet is real but not a 12-week-batch-derisked bet yet.

5. **The capex/circular-financing/rate-policy complex is the single biggest tail risk to the whole map.** $135B/quarter hyperscaler capex, $805B 2026 run-rate, Nvidia's $500B fund + $105B OpenAI guarantee, and softening bond demand (5x→<2x oversubscription since Feb) together mean a correction here — not Monastery-company-specific execution risk — is the most plausible single event that freezes the growth-equity market Monastery grads need to raise into. The Fed is now explicitly modeling AI capex as a monetary-policy variable (Jul 29 FOMC).

## Open watch items for next cycle

- Anthropic (~Oct 2026) and OpenAI (~Sept 2026, contested) IPOs — will make labs' true unit economics auditable for the first time; a hard test of "capabilities >> diffusion."
- GUARD Act floor vote (or continued stall) — determines whether federal preemption of the state patchwork arrives before 2027 effective dates stack up (CA live now; OR/WA/possibly NY effective Jan 1, 2027).
- Whether Nvidia's $500B fund / OpenAI Ohio guarantee draws SEC/accounting scrutiny beyond commentary — first real test of round-tripping concerns.
- Next RSI/continual-learning paper: watch for autonomous post-training attempted on a model >30B or on a frontier-class base, which would be the actual "slope changes" signal the GP thesis is watching for.
- Whether any Monastery-relevant seed-stage company emerges explicitly targeting the newly-identified 22-30-year-old AI-displaced-labor cohort as a *market*, not just a *risk*.
