## 1. The frame

**Thesis tested:** capabilities keep compounding at 2024–26 speed; economic diffusion takes ~10 years.

**Verdict: half right.** Aggregate diffusion *is* slow (Census: **18%** of US firms use AI in a business function; McKinsey: **<10%** have scaled agents in a given function; productivity **~2% y/y**). Continual learning **did not ship** — labs shipped memory *scaffolding* (Anthropic May, OpenAI June). RSI is real at the **scaffold** layer and bounded by **verification cost**.

**Wrong half is the investable half.** Diffusion is **bimodal**, not uniformly slow.

| Fast mode (12–24 months) | Slow mode (5–10 years) |
|---|---|
| Individual buyer, machine-verifiable output, no regulator | Institution, unverifiable quality, integration + law + physical presence |
| Cursor **$100M → $4B ARR in 18 months**; SpaceX closed **$60B** acquisition **14 Aug 2026** (8-K) | School districts, ministries, BPOs, licensed professions |
| Anthropic run-rate **~$65B** (end-Jul, Bloomberg, *not GAAP*); Claude **>80%** of Anthropic's own merged code | US firm AI use stuck **17–20%** for five months |

**Screening question for every Monastery company:** *Is there a cheap automatic evaluator, and who owns the outcome data?* METR: agents do **16–20h** tasks at 50% success, **3–4h** at the 80% commercial bar.

## 3. Education

**The price that governs the market:** school software clears at **$10–60/student/year** (Khanmigo/Broward = **$10**, waived). Tuition clears at **$10,000–75,000**. **200–1,000× is in becoming the provider.**

Three things changed in 24 months, none of them model quality:

1. **The obvious products are free.** ChatGPT for Teachers: **300k+ educators, 1 in 5 of America’s 20 largest districts**, FERPA, plugins **4 Aug 2026**. Google Gemini in Classroom on by default, all ages.
2. **District budgets are the worst since 2010.** ESSER ended early 2026; **6%** of states have sustainment plans (was 27%).
3. **Money moved to parents.** Texas ESAs: **101,600** students year one, 100k+ waitlist. Arizona: **102,188 (~10% of K-12)** on **~$1.1B/yr**. **~75,000 microschools, ~1.5M students**, median enrollment 20, no RFP.

**Engagement is unsolved.** Khanmigo: 40k → **700k** students, 45 → **380** districts — and Khan admits **only 15% with access regularly engage**. Synthesis (only SEC-filed consumer AI tutor): **4.5× subscribers → +7% revenue**, $959k cash vs $15.3M ST debt.

**Alef Education** (must-know sovereign comp): ADX-listed, H1’26 **AED 361.6M (~$98.5M)**, **74.7% EBITDA**, **+1.2% YoY**, ADEK contract through **2033**, Morocco expansion, **Alef AI for Teachers 11 Aug 2026**. Superb annuity, poor growth asset. Play is the layer they don’t own, or the *second* country.

**Alpha School / 2 Hour Learning:** 27 campuses opened **5 Aug 2026**, ~$40k tuition. Founders describe the “AI” as **adaptive practice software, not LLMs**. That is the tell.

**Monastery-stage names in-market (illustrative, not IC):** Medly AI, Wild Zebra, Sparkli, Miyagi Labs, Studdy, Maka Kids, Giant (kids interactive), CuePilot — curriculum-specific, outcome-measured, or parent/ESA channel. **Pass:** MagicSchool-class copilots (already competing with a free OpenAI plugin).

**Assessment is the quiet $1B+:** Stanford 7 → **50+** proctored courses; Princeton mandatory proctoring first time in **133 years**; Pearson **90% of profit** from Assessments & Verification / virtual schools / print. **AI-detection is dead. Handwriting OCR + oral/viva + process-evidence is live.**

## 4. Generative Netflix

**The 2023–25 thesis did not happen. 2026 evidence is actively negative.**

| Promise | 1 Sep 2026 |
|---|---|
| Sora as AI TikTok | **Dead.** OpenAI Help Center: app **26 Apr**, API **24 Sep**. Disney **$1B** character deal: **$0** changed hands |
| Personalized films per user | **Not shipped by anyone.** Coherence horizon of playable world models: **~60 seconds** (Genie 3) |
| Realtime cheap video | **Shipped** (24fps autoregressive). Still **~24× to ~1,300×** Netflix’s **~$0.08–0.26** revenue per view-hour |
| Generative NPCs everywhere | **Narrowly shipped:** Fortnite Conversations **publishable 30 Jul 2026**; NVIDIA ACE in ~8 titles |

**Where the money is (supply side, not demand side):**
- Kling **~$500M ARR**, **$18B** post (Jul 2026, secondary) — **50k enterprises**, ads + short-drama. Not consumer entertainment.
- Higgsfield Series B **$400M @ $5.4B (17 Aug)**. Claims **$700M annualized** — **self-reported run-rate**, DST/Goldman in the round, **not GAAP**. Treat as production-tooling, not Generative Netflix.
- Netflix: gen-AI in **~300 titles**; paid **$587M** for InterPositive (16 people). Cheaper conventional production, **zero** personalized-stream announcement.
- Audiobooks: **$200–500 → $5–22**/finished hour (**~35×**). **14%** of new US releases.

**Latitude** is the existence proof for the Monastery shape: **$7M+ ARR, profitable, <20 people**, five years / six engines. Moat = **deterministic world state**, not the model. Text is 3–4 orders of magnitude cheaper per engagement-hour than video.

**Fund:** state/memory/economy layer for UEFN + Roblox creators who just discovered their LLM NPCs have no memory; text/audio-native with a **published D30 vs Sora’s 1%**.  
**Do not fund:** “your film, generated as you watch.”

Long Lake / Amex GBT **$6.3B** take-private (shareholders **3 Aug**, close pending). Frontier labs now buy delivery (Ode + Anthropic: two consultancies in four months).

**The AI-readiness premium is already in the price.** Capstone Jul 2026: transformation is a **requirement, not a value enhancer**. MSP AI-enabled band **9–13×** vs **4–6×** baseline. Entry multiples that made the spreadsheet work (**3–4× EBITDA**) are gone.

Required VC uplift (Tenet survey): **3× EBITDA**. Bain: **~20%** of PE portcos have measurable gen-AI results; **7%** run fully autonomous agents in production.

## 6. Batch sourcing filter (print this)

**Yes, take the first meeting if all of these are true:**

1. Buyer is a **parent, ESA, microschool, licensed professional, or ministry** — not a US district RFP.
2. They **own an outcome** (tuition, recovery rate, contract closed, policy bound, exam validity) — not a seat license.
3. There is a **cheap evaluator** (next-item correctness, CPT accuracy, collection yield, clause-level redline) that compounds into proprietary data.
4. Big Tech **will not operate this** (building + liability, or a license, or 50-state child-safety, or a deterministic engine the lab will not maintain).
5. **$2M is the whole seed**, not a deposit on a $100M holdco.

**Hard pass:** wrappers on ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini; “we’ll roll up 40 agencies”; generative Netflix clones; AI detectors; anything whose D30, if published, would look like Sora.
