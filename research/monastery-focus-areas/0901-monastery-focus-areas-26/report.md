## Executive Summary

cyber•Fund’s Monastery writes **$2M uncapped SAFEs for 5%** into AI-native teams for 12 weeks (public terms, [cyber.fund](https://cyber.fund/); Batch 2 live). This session asked where that instrument should concentrate over five years, given a GP thesis that **AI will rewrite the economy, diffusion takes ~10 years, and capabilities keep compounding at 2024–26 speed**.

The thesis is **half right**. Aggregate adoption and measured TFP are slow. Continual learning did not ship. Recursive self-improvement is happening in **harnesses**, not in weights, and is gated by **verification**. The wrong half is the investable half: diffusion is **bimodal**. Where a practitioner has budget, output is machine-checkable, and there is no regulator, we just watched the fastest commercial diffusion on record (Cursor $100M→$4B ARR, SpaceX $60B close 14 Aug 2026; Anthropic ~$65B *run-rate* end-July). Where the buyer is an institution, quality is unverifiable, or a license is required, ten years is still the right prior.

That bimodality, plus the Monastery’s **check-size arithmetic**, decides the three markets:

1. **Education.** Do not sell chatbots to schools. OpenAI and Google priced the copilot and the general tutor at **$0**. Sell **tuition, ESA/microschool OS, or a regulated workflow** (assessment, IEP, sovereign curriculum). Alef proves the sovereign annuity (~$200M, 75% EBITDA, ~1% growth). Alpha proves the operator path. Khanmigo proves distribution without engagement (700k access, 15% regular use).
2. **Generative Netflix.** The 2023–25 consumer thesis **failed in 2026**. Sora is discontinued. Personalized video cannot amortize. Labs left for robotics/agents. Money accrued to **production tooling** (Kling, Higgsfield) and **cheaper conventional production** (Netflix, AI audiobooks). The Monastery-shaped remnant is a **deterministic state engine** for generative NPCs inside Fortnite/Roblox, and text/audio-native entertainment with a real D30.
3. **AI roll-ups.** The Oct 2025 call-center roll-up is **too late**. Buy-and-transform is a **$100M+ GC/Thrive** product. Monastery version: **AI-native licensed operator that owns the P&L without buying it** (Crosby: $5.8M→$60M B, zero acquisitions). Open verticals: collections, US real-estate services, staffing, wealth affiliation, specialty RCM, EU SME tax, MGAs.

**Preliminary view (portfolio construction):** Worth concentrating Monastery sourcing on **(A) parent/ESA/microschool learning engines that capture tuition or outcome data, (B) interactive-state software for UGC game platforms, (C) AI-native licensed services firms**. Likely pass on district copilots, generative Netflix clones, and acquisition holdcos.

## 1. Macro — capabilities vs diffusion

### What is true as of 1 Sep 2026

- **Rates:** Fed funds 3.50–3.75%; hike odds elevated into mid-September. AI capex is now a *monetary* variable (Warsh, Jackson Hole): if diffusion is slow, capex-demand wins and rates stay high — which punishes long-duration startups and rewards Monastery-shaped speed-to-revenue.
- **Labor:** Not economy-wide displacement. Stanford Digital Economy Lab (Aug 2026 ADP microdata): ages **22–25 in AI-exposed occupations 19% below** counterfactual (was 15% a year earlier). Mechanism = **reduced hiring**, not firing. White-collar payrolls have contracted ~31 months.
- **Lab economics vs firm adoption:** Anthropic ~$65B run-rate (Bloomberg, anonymous, end-July) vs OpenAI ~$40B; US firm use stuck ~18%. The capability-diffusion *gap is widening*, which is bullish for companies that sit in the fast mode and bearish for “waiting room” wrappers.
- **Continual learning:** Did not ship. Anthropic “dreaming” (May) and OpenAI “dreaming” (June) are memory-write behavior, not weight updates. Anthropic’s own Aug 14 risk report: failures recur even when the correction is in the memory file.
- **RSI:** Scaffold evolution (Darwin→Huxley→Mendel lineage) can be worth ~two orders of magnitude of parameters. METR expenditure-horizon work (21 Jul): after >$10k/run, GPT-5 and Opus-4.1 “only chase noise” on some research tasks. AlphaEvolve-class loops work where an automatic evaluator exists (chip, compiler, ranking).

### Investment implication

Monastery should **not** underwrite “AGI in 2027 therefore education/entertainment/services are one trade.” Underwrite **verification ownership** in slow-diffusing domains, and **workflow ownership** where the model is already good enough and the institution has not redesigned the job (McKinsey: ~39% report EBIT impact, ~21% redesigned a workflow).

## 3. Generative Netflix

### What failed

OpenAI discontinued Sora’s consumer app **26 Apr 2026** and the API **24 Sep 2026** ([Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation)). Lifetime in-app revenue **$2.1M** vs ~**$1M/day** inference; **1% D30**. Disney’s $1B character licence **never funded**. Fable/Showrunner raised a **$5.4M Khosla seed (3 Jul)** and repositioned to a developer platform. Decart’s playable world pivoted toward AV simulation (Anthropic talks at $6–7B are **unconfirmed**). Luma opened a robotics lab. Runway moved to world models/avatars.

### Arithmetic

Netflix amortizes across hundreds of billions of view-hours (**~$0.08–0.26** revenue per hour). Cheapest usable offline gen-video is **orders of magnitude** above that; even realtime Helios-class streams are tens of times Netflix ARPU. **Per-user generation has denominator 1.** Cost curves help production tooling; they do not create personalized SVOD in this planning window.

Realtime **pixels** arrived in 2026 (24fps, multi-minute). **Story** did not (Genie 3 ~60s sessions). Character consistency ~0.74 vs a 1.0 physics/identity bar. That is the research gate for 2027–28.

### Where value accrued

Kling ~**$500M ARR** / **$18B** (secondary, Jul). Higgsfield **$400M Series B @ $5.4B (17 Aug)** with a **self-reported $700M annualized** — treat as directional proof of **prosumer/enterprise production**, not consumer PMF. Netflix gen-AI in ~300 titles; InterPositive **$587M**. Audiobook narration **~35×** cheaper; 14% of new US releases.

Catalogs: `generative-netflix/companies-video-film.md` (~47), `companies-games.md` (~51). Tech: `generative-netflix/tech-realtime.md`. Market: `generative-netflix/market.md`.

### Investment lens

- **View:** **Likely pass** on the named thesis. **Worth exploring** the **state layer for generative play** (Latitude shape; Fortnite Conversations went publishable **30 Jul 2026**) and text/audio-native with a D30 that beats 1%.
- **Big Tech threat:** Google already ships model + world model + NPC brain + distribution rules inside Fortnite. Defensibility is **deterministic software, licensed data, or inference cost**, not a prompt.

## 5. Key findings

### Strengths of the opportunity set

1. Capability-diffusion **gap is measurable** (METR 50% vs 80% horizons; Census vs lab ARR).
2. Education has a **clean price discontinuity** (software $10–60 vs tuition $10k–75k) and a **new budget holder** (ESA/parent).
3. Generative entertainment has a **falsified consumer thesis**, which is useful — it prevents a bad batch.
4. Roll-ups have a **working seed archetype** (Crosby) distinct from the PE trade.

### Concerns

1. Many load-bearing private prints are **run-rate / self-reported** (Anthropic $65B, Higgsfield $700M, GC margin claims).
2. Monastery’s **uncapped SAFE + 5%** is ambiguous if a company later prices a $300M AI Series A (Carta Q1 2026 median pre for AI-foundational). Resolve legally before Batch 2.
3. Child-safety and education regulation (COPPA amendments **22 Apr 2026**, EU AI Act, Character.AI settlements) can kill 0–6 products overnight (Moxie precedent).
4. If a lab ships true continual learning or 20-minute coherent video in 2027, slides 3–4 change. Track those two gates.

### Open questions

- Does Alpha’s public-school pilot (Houston ISD / Springfield) survive contact with unions and NAEP, or only $40k private tuition?
- Will Fortnite/Roblox creators pay for a third-party state engine, or will Epic/Roblox absorb it?
- First audited same-store AI margin print from GC/Thrive — when, and does it look like 33% or 4×?
