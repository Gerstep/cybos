## Take-Privates and Failures

### One Medical (Amazon, 2023) — Expensive Cautionary Tale
- Amazon paid **$3.9B** (Feb 2023) — ~18× revenue; company was deeply unprofitable
- Pre-acquisition: $420M operating loss (2022)
- Post-acquisition: Projected $500M+ operating loss 2024; Amazon's Project Espresso forced $100M+ in cuts, mass layoffs, office closures
- **Lesson**: Premium membership concierge (100+ physician practices) requires massive capital to scale. Unit economics don't work until panel fully mature. Not a lean startup.

### Forward Health (Shutdown, Nov 2024)
- Raised **$650M+** total; $100M Series E just 12 months before shutdown
- Model: CarePod kiosks ($149/mo), $2–3M/clinic buildout, in-house hardware
- **Failure**: Hardware technical failures, broken blood draws, pulled lab tests, high CAC, low retention, ran out of cash
- **Lesson**: Physical capital-intensive rollout needs unit economics proof before scale. Hardware adds risk without adding defensibility.

### Babylon Health (Bankruptcy, Aug 2023)
- SPAC 2021 @ $4.1B. Raised $1.2B+ total. Filed Chapter 7 bankruptcy.
- **Failure**: SPAC revenue expectations unachievable; US expansion before tech was mature; high PAC, low reimbursement
- **Lesson**: SPAC healthcare companies overvalued narrative over traction. Value-based care contracts require true clinical efficacy.

### Cerebral (Near-Death, Survived via pivot)
- Peak valuation: **$4.8B** (2021). Total raised: $462M.
- **Crisis**: DOJ/DEA investigation for gamifying stimulant Rx rates (2019–2022); FTC $7M settlement (Apr 2024); DOJ $6.57M settlement (Nov 2024); HIPAA breach 3.2M patients
- Stopped controlled substances entirely Oct 2022; mass layoffs; rebuilt as insurance-based therapy platform
- **Lesson**: Prescribing telehealth at scale creates regulatory surface area. Metrics that incentivize Rx volume = DOJ risk. Mental health at scale needs clinical governance, not growth-hacking.

## Typical Seed–A by Model

### Cash-Pay Single-Location Concierge Clinic
- **Seed**: $2–8M @ $8–20M pre-money
- **Series A**: $10–30M @ $40–80M post (after 2–3 profitable locations)
- **Revenue**: $3,200 median fee × 300–500 members = $960k–$1.6M/physician; 35–50% overhead

### AI-Native Preventive Membership (DTC)
- **Seed**: $5–16M @ $20–60M pre-money (AI premium ~+42% per Carta 2024)
- **Series A**: $20–50M @ $60–200M; Superpower $30M @ $300M is an outlier
- **Examples**: Prosper Medical ($16M seed, Jul 2026); SONATA ($7M seed, Jul 2026); Mito Health ($2M seed, Mar 2025, YC-backed)

### Peptide / Compounding Telehealth
- **Seed**: $3–16M (Protocole $6M, NoHo Labs $16M)
- **Series A**: $15–40M (regulatory clarity required first)
- **ARPU**: $720/yr membership + $1,600–2,400/yr peptides = $2,300–3,100/member combined

### Mental Health / Coaching — B2B SaaS (New Focus)
- **Seed**: $5–15M
- **Series A**: $17–50M (Ease Health $41M a16z, Onos $17M Costanoa)
- **Note**: Consumer therapy apps are out of favor; B2B behavioral health infrastructure is where the checks go now

## Unit Economics Benchmarks

| Model | ARPU/yr | Gross Margin | 5-yr LTV (90% ret.) | Benchmark CAC | LTV/CAC |
|-------|---------|-------------|---------------------|--------------|---------|
| DPC ($75–150/mo) | $900–1,800 | 55–65% | $4k–8k | $50–150 | 30–80x |
| Classic concierge (entry) | $2,000–3,500 | 45–60% | $9k–16k | $300–800 | 20–40x |
| AI preventive ($199–365/yr) | $199–365 | 60–75% | $800–1,500 | $20–80 | 15–40x |
| Premium preventive ($2,500/yr) | $2,500 | 50–65%* | $10k–15k | $200–500 | 25–50x |
| Peptide membership + product | $2,300–3,100 | 40–60% | $8k–14k | $150–400 | 20–50x |
| Insurance concierge hybrid | $828 fee + insurance | ~25–35% blended | $3k–8k | $300–600 | 10–20x |
| Ultra-premium concierge | $15k–50k+ | 55–70% | $50k–200k+ | $1k–5k | 30–80x |

**Key**: Cash-pay preventive has best LTV/CAC economics at scale (low CAC with organic waitlists). Insurance-billed models have lower sticker but margin compression from claims. Ultra-premium is high-LTV but tiny TAM (cannot build a $1B company serving 1,000 patients at $50k/yr).

**DPC tailwind (2026)**: One Big Beautiful Bill Act (Jan 2026) makes DPC fees HSA-eligible up to $150/mo. Removes prior disqualification. Meaningful adoption driver for HDHP population.

## Monastery Filter: $2M / 5% / ~$40M Post

**The math**: $2M on 5% = ~$40M post-money implied.

### Fits ($40M post, seed-stage, 12-week AI transformation):
1. **AI-guided lab marketplace** — orchestrates Quest/Labcorp panels + AI interpretation; no owned infrastructure. Think "Superpower pre-seed stage" or "Rupa Health for longevity consumers." Capital: $2M covers 6–12 months. AI unlock: personalized panel recommendations + result coaching. Comparable: Mito Health (YC S25, $2M seed).

2. **Peptide protocol platform (software-only)** — clinician matching + protocol library + 503A pharmacy routing. No pharmacy ownership. Comparable: Protocole ($6M seed, Apr 2026). $40M post is right.

3. **AI concierge layer for independent DPC/concierge physicians** — helps solo/boutique physicians acquire members, automate care plans, monetize AI protocols. Sells software to physicians (B2B), not patients directly. Avoids medical practice ownership complexity.

4. **Specialty telehealth (narrow vertical)** — e.g., AI-first fertility navigation, longevity protocol coaching, executive performance medicine. Cash-pay, tight TAM, no insurance. Single founder with clinical credentials + AI can run this.

5. **Behavioral health clinical AI** — not consumer therapy, but practice-facing: AI for session notes, care plans, outcome tracking. Comparable to Ease Health but at earlier stage. $40M post is right for pre-product.

### Does NOT fit:
- Physical clinic roll-outs (capital-intensive; Forward Health trap)
- Insurance-billed primary care network (One Medical never worked lean)
- Compounding pharmacy ownership (FDA licensing + capital; not a 12-week build)
- Full-stack longevity clinics at scale (Neko-style requires $100M+ before US works)
- Consumer genomics (23andMe failure shows consumer standalone DNA is a dead end)

### The Monastery play:
**AI coordination software** on top of existing healthcare infrastructure (labs, pharmacies, physicians). Own the patient relationship and interpretation layer. $2M is enough for 6–12 months of product + first 500–2,000 members. 12 weeks builds the AI differentiation (personalized protocol engine, longitudinal health context, care navigation agent). Exit: acqui-hire by Quest, Labcorp, Hims, or Function — or Series A at $80–150M with 5,000+ members and $1M+ ARR.
