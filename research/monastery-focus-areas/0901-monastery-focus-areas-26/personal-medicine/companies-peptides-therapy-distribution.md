## THE REGULATORY MOMENT (CONTEXT FOR ALL COMPANIES)

The entire US personalized medicine landscape was transformed by two events in 2025–2026:

**Event 1 — GLP-1 crackdown**: FDA resolved semaglutide (Feb 2025) and tirzepatide (Dec 2024) shortages, ending mass-market compounding. 135+ warning letters. Hims/Ro/Noom pivoted. Gray-market vendors closed. Enforcement ongoing.

**Event 2 — Peptide PCAC vote (Jul 23–24, 2026)**: FDA advisory committee voted 8-6 to recommend BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, MOTS-c, Semax, Epitalon for 503A bulks list — **against FDA staff recommendation**. This is **non-binding**. No final rule. Pharmacies cannot compound them today. Rulemaking = 12–18 months minimum.

**Combined effect**: The gray market is closing. The licensed market is opening (slowly). Companies positioned at the intersection of telehealth + licensed compounding + AI-personalization have a structural window **now through 2028**.

## SECTION K: EU PEPTIDE REGULATION

| Country | BPC-157/TB-500 compounding | Status |
|---------|---------------------------|--------|
| Germany | Prohibited (AMG §73) | Active customs enforcement; criminal risk for import |
| Austria | Prohibited (AMG-AT equivalent) | Less aggressive than Germany |
| UK | Prohibited (Human Medicines Regs 2012) | MHRA ignores "research use" labels; moderate enforcement |

| France/Italy/Spain | Prohibited | Lower enforcement; magistral compounding requires recognized API |

## MONASTERY INVESTMENT FILTER

### Question: Can a $2M seed own a 503A/clinic network, or only software into it?

**Short answer**: **Only software into it** at seed stage. **Acquire the pharmacy at Series A** once telehealth ARR proves model.

### Decision Matrix

| Play | $2M viable? | AI leverage | $1B+ path | Comparable |
|------|------------|------------|----------|------------|
| Build 503A pharmacy from scratch | No — $500K–$2M cleanroom alone; 12–18 months compliance | Low | No | N/A |
| Acquire small 503A (~$500K SDE) | Borderline — $1.75–3M acquisition + SBA 70% | Operational | Only with roll-up | Noom/TMC at PE scale |
| AI-native telehealth + 503A supplier | **Yes** — software + supply relationship | High | Yes — Hone/Maximus model | Hone Health, Maximus |
| AI copilot for clinic operators | Yes — SaaS | High | Low — fragmented B2B | Point solution |
| Premium offline longevity clinic | No — $2–5M capex per location | Low at seed | PE-required | Fountain Life, Next Health |
| Peptide telehealth (post-PCAC) | Yes — **2027–2028 window** | High | Yes | Maximus GHRH play |
| Diagnostic-only (Function model) | No — Quest contracts, phlebotomy network | Medium | Crowded | Function Health |

### Best $2M Monastery Seed Plays (Ranked)

1. **AI-native telehealth platform, specific condition vertical** — Hone/Maximus model; $129–149/month subscription; at-home lab; mail Rx via 503A supplier. 5,000 patients → $8–9M ARR → Series A. Key insight: peptides (sermorelin, CJC-1295) are already on the 503A bulks list; BPC-157 likely follows in 2027.

2. **Peptide-specific telehealth** (injury recovery / gut / performance) timed to FDA rulemaking. Build the platform; plug in BPC-157 etc. as legality unlocks. First-mover in legal peptide distribution = Maximus-style defensible position.

3. **AI-guided protocol personalization for longevity clinics** — SaaS or embedded AI copilot (biomarker interpretation, protocol generation, follow-up automation) for Next Health, Fountain Life, Restore fragmented chains. Small initial contract values; path to data moat if longitudinal outcomes data can be captured.

### What is PE/GC Territory (Not Seed)

- 503A roll-up at scale: $100M+ capital; 3–5 year integration; high regulatory risk
- Premium longevity clinic chain: $5M+ per clinic; requires branded real estate + clinical staff
- Franchise wellness roll-up (Restore model): General Atlantic-type capital ($140M)
- Large diagnostic platform (Function model): $800M+ required

### Key Risk Flag

503A pharmacies that pivot from GLP-1 to peptides face:
1. **Regulatory risk**: BPC-157/TB-500 still illegal today; PCAC vote is advisory only
2. **Enforcement risk**: State boards actively inspecting; Lilly/Novo litigation continuing
3. **Quality risk**: USP 797/800 compliance expensive to maintain; FDA warning letters catastrophic

Any Monastery company **must not own or operate a pharmacy at seed** — that's the compliance liability risk that kills a small company. Own the patient relationship + prescription flow; use licensed 503A as an arm's-length supplier.
