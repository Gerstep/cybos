## SEGMENT D — AI THERAPY BAN LANDSCAPE (REGULATORY)

**Key laws (as of Sept 2026)**:

| State | Law | Effective | Key Prohibition |
|-------|-----|-----------|-----------------|
| Illinois | Wellness & Oversight for Psychological Resources (WOPR) Act, HB 1806 | Aug 4, 2025 | AI may not provide/advertise therapy unless licensed professional conducts; AI cannot make independent therapeutic decisions, directly interact therapeutically, or generate unreviewed treatment plans. Up to $10K/violation. |
| Nevada | Similar to Illinois WOPR | July 20, 2026 | Mirrors Illinois framework |
| Rhode Island | R.I. Gen. Laws ch. 40.1-5.5 | July 20, 2026 | AI therapy prohibition; licensed professional required |
| Maine | Companion law | July 20, 2026 | AI therapy prohibition |
| New York | AI Companion Models Law | Nov 5, 2025 | NOT a therapy ban — requires AI disclosure + crisis referral protocol for companion bots. $15K/day penalty. |
| California | SB 243 | Jan 1, 2026 | Companion chatbot rules: crisis protocol, AI disclosure, minor protections, private right of action ($1,000+/violation). |
| Nebraska | LB 525 | 2025 | Mental health + companion chatbot rules |

**Federal**: FTC 6(b) study orders (Sept 2025) to Alphabet, Character Technologies, Instagram, Meta, OpenAI, Snap, xAI — studying chatbot harms to children. Findings expected 2026/27. No federal ban yet.

**Medicare reimbursement**: CMS created G-codes G0552/G0553/G0554 (Jan 1, 2025 PFS) for FDA-cleared Digital Mental Health Treatments (DMHTs). First national Medicare coverage for prescription digital therapeutics like SleepioRx, DaylightRx.

**Key insight for Monastery**: The WOPR framework kills pure AI-therapy chatbots in 4 states with more likely to follow. The surviving models all have "licensed professional in the loop." FDA-cleared DMHT designation is the compliance moat for digital therapeutics.

## KEY THEMATIC ANALYSIS

### What Works: Employer Benefits Networks vs. DTC Chatbots

**Employer benefits networks WIN on:**
- Revenue durability: B2B contracts are multi-year, high ACV ($50K–$5M+/employer)
- Regulatory compliance: Licensed clinician delivery satisfies WOPR-style laws
- Insurance integration: Network credentialing creates payer moat
- Outcomes data: Employer expects ROI → drives outcomes measurement → data flywheel
- **Evidence**: Spring Health ($1B target ARR, profitable), Grow Therapy ($1B ARR), Lyra ($243M ARR) — all growing

**DTC Cash-Pay Chatbots FAIL on:**
- Cash-pay collapse: BetterHelp –12.7% to –19% 2026. Talkspace consumer –25.4%
- Regulatory exposure: 4 states ban AI therapy; more coming; liability suits (Character.AI)
- No insurance moat: Entire market shifting toward insurance coverage
- **Evidence**: Done Global criminal conviction; Replika €5M fine; Character.AI teen suicide settlements

**The winning structural model (2026):**
> Licensed clinician network → AI handles triage/matching/admin/between-session support → insurance reimbursement → employer benefits contract as primary distribution channel

### Moat: Licensed Clinician Network + Outcomes vs. Model Wrapper

**Tier 1 moat (hard to replicate in 12 weeks):**
- Insurance credentialing + payer contracts (2–5 year build)
- Clinician network (quality screening, state licensing)
- Clinical outcomes data (years of session data)
- FDA clearance (DMHT pathway: SleepioRx, Woebot, Wysa)
- Employer/health plan relationships

**Tier 2 moat (buildable but expensive):**
- Consumer brand trust (Headspace brand = 15 years)
- Clinical protocol + EHR infrastructure
- AI trained on proprietary session data (Talkspace Sentia LLM)

**Zero moat (model wrapper):**
- General LLM + therapy-ish prompt = WOPR violation + ChatGPT feature tomorrow
- Consumer DTC cash-pay subscription without insurance = structurally declining
