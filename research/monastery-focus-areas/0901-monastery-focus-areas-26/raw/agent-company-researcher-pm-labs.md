## SECTION 8: FAILURES / CAUTIONARY TALES

### 42. 23andMe (bankrupt → nonprofit)
- **Status**: Bankrupt; assets sold
- **Peak valuation**: $6B (2021 SPAC)
- **Cause of failure**: Data breach (7M customers, 2023); loss of GSK drug discovery partnership; co-founder Anne Wojcicki board fight; collapse in consumer genomics standalone business; failed Total Health subscription product
- **Outcome**: Chapter 11 filed Mar 23 2025; auction (Regeneron won first auction $256M, then TTAM at $305M); TTAM Research Institute (Wojcicki nonprofit) acquired for $305M (Dec 2025 effective). Genetic data of 15M customers transferred to nonprofit
- **Lesson**: Consumer genomics ($99 spit test) without medical utility, drug discovery revenue, or subscription lock-in is not a standalone business at scale; data breach creates existential privacy risk; SPAC overvaluation

### 43. Invitae (sold/wound down)
- **Status**: Sold to Labcorp 2024; operations wound down
- **Peak**: $8B+ valuation (2021); "democratize genetics" thesis
- **Cause of failure**: Unsustainable cash burn; aggressive testing price reduction (raced to the bottom); inability to achieve profitability; reimbursement battles; competition from larger genomics players and free/low-cost carrier screening. $300M+ annual losses
- **Outcome**: Labcorp acquired assets (genetic testing menu, some technology); former employees and test infrastructure absorbed
- **Lesson**: Commodity genomics without clinical differentiation or sustainable unit economics is fatal; racing to the bottom on price while burning $300M/year fails even if mission is right

## WHAT WORKS vs. VAPOR (SYNTHESIS)

**WORKS (proven, recurring, real demand):**
- Subscription blood panels with 100-160 biomarkers + AI interpretation + Quest/Labcorp draw access: Function ($365/year, 500K members), Superpower ($199-349, Series A). Proven PMF, growing fast
- Vertically integrated with pharmacy: LetsGetChecked (end-to-end — collect → lab → Rx). Real clinical utility
- CRC stool DNA (Cologuard): $3.2B revenue under Abbott; reimbursed; highest-evidence cancer screening
- Shield CRC blood test: $218-230M FY 2026 guidance; Medicare-covered; physician-ordered
- National lab networks (Quest, Labcorp): unassailable infrastructure; AI adding margin not disruption
- Genomics backbone (Natera $2.85B, GeneDx $475M, Tempus $1.6B): real physician orders, real insurance revenue
- Supplement + testing bundle: Thorne ($500M+) and IM8 ($210M, 19-month brand)

**VAPOR (unproven/burning cash/skepticism warranted):**
- Multi-cancer early detection without reimbursement: Galleri losing $100M+/quarter; no broad CMS coverage yet
- Full-body MRI + AI doctor for everyone: Fountain Life ($10K-85K/year), HLI ($8K-25K) — tiny TAM, no insurance, no clinical validation RCTs
- Consumer genomics standalone: 23andMe bankrupt at $305M; Invitae wound down
- Microbiome recommendations at scale: Viome ($25-50M revenue after $301M raised; RNA sequencing genuinely novel but recommendation quality disputed)

**PIVOT STORIES:**
- InsideTracker: B2C → B2B platform (Terra) — structurally correct but execution unclear
- Prenetics: genomics → supplements (IM8) — pivot worked commercially ($210M ARR) but no longer diagnostics
