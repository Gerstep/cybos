## A. AI NPC / Character Platforms (dialogue, voice, behavior infra)

1. **Inworld AI** (SF/Palo Alto, founded 2021) — Character Engine → pivoted to broader "voice AI infrastructure" (TTS ranked #1 on latency benchmarks, STT, LLM router, Realtime API). Raised $130M+ total, including $50M round in 2023 at ~$500M valuation, plus a further $50M round reported July 2026 (sources conflict on whether valuation held at $500M or moved). Investors: Lightspeed, Founders Fund, Kleiner Perkins, Intel Capital, Samsung Next, Microsoft/Xbox partnership (co-developed narrative design tool). **Shipped**: Mecha BREAK (Amazing Seasun Games) deploys Inworld NPCs in a released game; Covert Protocol is a tech demo (NVIDIA/Inworld), not a shipped game. Ubisoft, Niantic engagements are prototypes/POCs, not shipped full titles. Cost structure: per-API-call voice/text credits — "success tax" dynamic (cost scales with the exact engagement AI is meant to drive). *Sources: TechCrunch, Indexed.vc, aigamingdev.com, veriprajna.com (Jan 2026 GlobeNewswire cites AI-NPC-behavior market at $1.41B in 2024 → $5.51B by 2029, 31.2% CAGR).*

2. **Convai** (San Jose, founded 2022 by ex-NVIDIA engineer Purnendu Mukherjee + Prashant Mishra) — 3D-aware conversational AI NPCs with multimodal scene perception (sight+hearing), integrates Unreal/Unity/Roblox/NVIDIA ACE/XR runtimes. Raised only ~$5M seed (Oct 2022, Dune Ventures, Monta Vista Capital, Rainfall Ventures) — reported ~$6.5M revenue in 2024, ~41 employees. Notably small funding relative to Inworld despite comparable tech scope — capital-efficient but may lack war chest for a compute-cost war.

3. **Charisma.ai** (UK/Austria, founded 2016) — "World's first AI voice-driven video games studio" positioning; story-graph-controlled generative improvisation (claims "zero gen-AI hallucinations" via guardrails), SDKs for Unity/Unreal/web. Tiny team (1-10 employees, -20% YoY as of Jan 2026) — a survivor, not a scaler. Used for games, training sims, brand experiences.

4. **NVIDIA ACE (Avatar Cloud Engine)** — Not a startup; NVIDIA's suite of on-device + cloud AI models (ASR, SLM, TTS, Audio2Face) for game NPCs, positioned as infra layer under third-party NPC platforms. **Shipped in real games** (the clearest "actually shipped" evidence in this space): (a) **PUBG Ally** (KRAFTON) — co-playable character (CPC) in public beta since June 17 2026, on-device 2B-param Mistral-NeMo-Minitron SLM, runs on GPUs with as little as 8GB VRAM; (b) **Total War: PHARAOH** (Creative Assembly) — experimental AI advisor using local SLM + RAG over 1,200+ game data tables, playtest 2026; (c) **inZOI** (Krafton) — "Smart Zoi" on-device SLM-driven autonomous NPC personalities, still experimental/high system requirements as of 2026; (d) **The Oversight Bureau** (Iconic Interactive) — ACE-powered narrative engine with on-device Riva ASR. Pattern: ACE adoption skews toward **on-device small language models**, explicitly to avoid the cloud-cost/latency problem plaguing Inworld-style cloud NPCs.

5. **Player2 / Elefant AI** (founded 2024 by Wenzhe Shi, ex-ShareChat) — "Drop a brain into any game character" toolkit; free, handles LLM/voice/server costs so indie devs don't pay cloud bills directly (unclear how this is sustainable — likely subsidized/pre-monetization). ~30K MAU, ~10K Discord members reported mid-2025. **Failed to reach independent scale**: founder + several team members left to join Roblox in May 2026 to build AI NPC/agent tech inside Roblox instead — a talent-acquisition-style outcome rather than an exit, and a signal that platform-native NPC AI (built by Roblox itself) may out-compete standalone NPC middleware.

6. **ElevenLabs** (voice infra, adjacent) — $781M+ raised, $11B valuation (2026), $330M+ ARR; used by Epic Games and Meta among others for narrative dialogue/localization. Positioned as the stable, well-funded alternative after PlayAI's collapse (see below). Real-time latency still a constraint for live NPC dialogue vs. pre-generated narrative lines.

7. **PlayAI / PlayHT — FAILED/ACQUIHIRED**: Acquired by Meta July 12, 2025 (~35-person team absorbed into Superintelligence Labs); API shut down July 26, 2025; entire platform permanently terminated Dec 31, 2025 with **no data migration path** — all user accounts, voice clones, and API integrations deleted. Had real enterprise revenue (Samsung, Xerox, Amazon, Volvo) at time of acquisition — this was an acquihire, not a failure of product-market fit, but it left every downstream game studio with a hard-dependency outage. **Direct lesson for Monastery-stage bets on infra vendors: platform/API dependency risk is real and can go to zero overnight with no warning window.**

## B. AI-Native Narrative / Interactive-Fiction Games

8. **Latitude** (AI Dungeon, and new "Voyage") — AI Dungeon (2019) pioneered LLM-based infinite text-adventure gaming, millions of players, tens of thousands of creators. **Voyage** (unveiled April 21 2026, opened to everyone Aug 26 2026 on iOS/Android/web, no waitlist) is the "second generation": adds a proprietary **World Engine** — a deterministic layer (skill checks, HP, permadeath, inventory, relationships) sitting atop the improvisational LLM, explicitly to solve the "AI can't hallucinate the world state" problem that pure-LLM games like AI Dungeon suffer from. Uses Google Gemini Flash for generation + Gemma for smaller tasks. Backed by NFX, Google's AI Futures Fund, Griffin Gaming Partners, Album VC. Subscription tiers $14.99–$99.99/mo across both products. 160,000+ unique AI-generated characters interacted with, ~3,000 average actions per beta tester. **Notable architecture lesson**: the market is converging on "LLM for improvisation + deterministic engine for state" rather than pure end-to-end generation — the same pattern NVIDIA/Krafton use (SLM + RAG/rules), not coincidence.

9. **Hidden Door** (Brooklyn, founded 2020, $9M total funding — Seed $7M led by Makers Fund 2022, Pre-seed $2M led by Northzone 2020) — AI storytelling/social-roleplay platform: turns public-domain works (Pride and Prejudice, Call of Cthulhu, Wizard of Oz) into infinite collaborative roleplay ("interactive graphic novel" format). Licenses IP via creator revenue-share rather than training on unlicensed work. Launched **Atlas** worldbuilding tool (May 2026) letting users build and monetize their own interactive worlds — 30% of platform subscription revenue shared with world creators. Small team (10-20 employees) but has sustained multi-year cadence of weekly feature ships (avatar customization, species archetypes, profile pages) — evidence of a real, if small, live-service product, not a demo.

10. **NovelAI** (Anlatan LLC, Wilmington DE, founded 2021) — **Zero VC funding, fully subscription-funded** — the sharpest capital-efficiency case study in this catalog. ~2.5M+ total users, ~8.1M monthly visits (Nov 2025), 15+ min avg session. Combines fiction-tuned text model (Kayra 13B / Erato 70B / GLM-4.6 / a 355B MoE called "Xialong") with an anime-style image diffusion model (Anime V4) in one product. Pricing $10-25/mo. ~$5M estimated ARR. Emerged from AI Dungeon refugees fleeing that platform's 2021 content-moderation crackdown — a direct example of an incumbent's policy misstep creating a venture-scale (if bootstrapped) competitor.

11. **KoboldAI** — Free, open-source interface layer connecting users to local or cloud LLMs for uncensored text generation; no subscription, users bring their own compute/API keys. The "self-hosted" end of the spectrum vs. NovelAI's "turnkey cloud" end — illustrates the barbell in this market between fully-hosted paid products and free/open tooling for technical users.

12. **Character.AI** (entertainment/roleplay use case; excluding any edu framing per market split) — Founded by Noam Shazeer + Daniel De Freitas (Nov 2021); Google paid ~$2.7B in an Aug 2024 licensing-and-talent deal (founders returned to Google DeepMind), implying ~$10B valuation without a formal acquisition. 2026 metrics (sources conflict): 20-45M MAU depending on tracker, ~165-185M monthly visits, ~$32-60M annual revenue (up from $30M in 2024, ~$50M in 2025 per Business of Apps). Monetization shifted from pure subscription (c.ai+, $10/mo) to a "Roblox-ification" virtual economy (**Charms** micro-currency launched early 2026) plus advertising during character-switch moments. **Notable 2026 pivot**: launched **c.ai Series** (July 2026) — in-house-produced short-form vertical microdramas where viewers can chat with the characters after each episode, explicitly testing "the character, not the episode, is the durable asset" thesis, and opening production tools to outside creators (platform-model ambitions). **Also the sharpest child-safety cautionary tale in the sector**: multiple wrongful-death/harm lawsuits (FL, TX, CO, NY) alleging chatbot-contributed suicides of two minors; Character.AI + Google settled all filed cases by Jan 7 2026 (terms undisclosed) after banning open-ended chat for under-18 users (Nov 2025).

13. **Series Entertainment** (formerly Series AI; SF, founded 2023 by Pany Haritatos, ex-Kongregate/Snap games exec) — Building **Rho Engine**, self-described "first AI-native, multimodal full-stack game creation platform" (vs. Modl.ai's narrower QA focus or Unity Muse's assistant framing). $35.9M total funding ($7.9M seed led by a16z 2023 → $28M Series A Sept 2024, Netflix + Dell Technologies Capital + a16z/BITKRAFT/F4 follow-on). **Acquired Pixelberry Studios** (July 2024, maker of *Choices: Stories You Play*) to get an existing interactive-fiction catalog + audience onto Rho — an AI-tools company buying a traditional studio for distribution, the inverse of the more common "studio buys AI tool" pattern. Team grew 17 → 120+ post-acquisition.

14. **Fable Studio / Showrunner** (SF, founded 2018 by Edward Saatchi + Pete Billington, ex-Oculus Story Studio) — Not a game per se but the clearest "generative Netflix ↔ generative game" hybrid: **SHOW-2** model generates full animated TV episodes from text prompts inside persistent shared story-worlds (Exit Valley, Ikiru Shinu, Sim Francisco). Public alpha since July 2025 (10,000-user closed alpha before that), lives mostly on Discord. Backed by Amazon's Alexa Fund + Day One Ventures, 8VC, Greycroft. Free during alpha; planned $10-40/mo subscription + creator revenue-share. In licensing talks with Disney (no signed deal as of early 2026) — the IP-holder-partnership model this whole "generative Netflix" thesis depends on remains unproven at deal-close stage. First went viral generating **unauthorized South Park episodes** (2023) — an IP-risk cautionary tale baked into the company's own origin story.

## C. World Models — Research Labs and Frontier AI Labs

15. **Google DeepMind — Genie 3 / Project Genie** — Announced Aug 5 2025; consumer-facing **Project Genie** launched on Google Labs Jan 29 2026 (Google AI Ultra subscribers, US-only, 18+). Generates real-time, promptable, navigable environments from text/image/Street View grounding at 720p/24fps; "promptable world events" let users alter weather/objects mid-generation; consistency holds for "several minutes." Explicitly framed by DeepMind as "a key stepping stone on the path to AGI." Not a persistent game engine — sessions are bounded (~60 seconds per generation in some product framings).

16. **Google DeepMind — SIMA 2** (Nov 13 2025) — Generalist embodied agent (Scalable Instructable Multiworld Agent), core reasoning now driven by **Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite**; perceives pixels, acts via virtual keyboard/mouse, no game-code API access. Roughly doubles SIMA 1's task-completion rate (31%→62% in ASKA, 42%→68% in MineDojo vs. ~71% human baseline). Self-improvement loop: Gemini generates tasks + estimates rewards, agent refines via trial-and-error. Being tested inside **EVE Online** (partnership with Fenris Creations, announced Aug 21 2026) — DeepMind's 15-year games-research arc culminating in a live commercial MMO as testbed. Limited research preview only; long-horizon task performance and short context-window memory remain explicit weaknesses.

17. **Microsoft Research — Muse / WHAM (World and Human Action Models)** — Developed with Xbox Game Studios' Ninja Theory, trained on 1B+ images/actions (7+ years of continuous gameplay) from *Bleeding Edge*. Published in *Nature* (2025). Evolved into **WHAM-RT**, a real-time playable version demoed on Quake II gameplay via Copilot Labs (May 2026). Explicitly framed as "gameplay ideation" tooling for developers (informed by interviews with 27 game creators from indie to AAA), not a consumer product — Microsoft is the most research-cautious of the Big Tech world-model efforts, releasing weights/data openly on Azure AI Foundry rather than shipping a consumer app.

18. **GameNGen** (Google Research, 2024 paper, not a company) — First neural-network-only game engine: simulates DOOM in real time (20+ fps on a single TPU) via a two-phase process (RL agent generates gameplay data → fine-tuned Stable Diffusion v1.4 predicts next frame). PSNR 29.4 (JPEG-comparable); human raters barely better than chance at spotting simulated clips. Pure research artifact — no product, no company — but the seminal proof-of-concept the whole "diffusion world model = neural game engine" category cites.

19. **DIAMOND (Diffusion As a Model Of eNvironment Dreams)** — NeurIPS 2024 spotlight research (Eloi Alonso, Adam Jelley, Vincent Micheli). Diffusion world model + RL agent trained inside it; 1.46 mean human-normalized score on Atari-100k (first world-model-trained agent to cross 1.0). Demonstrated a standalone interactive CS:GO neural engine trained on just 87 hours of gameplay (~0.5% of the data GameNGen needed) — the paper's core claim is that diffusion (vs. discrete-token) world models are dramatically more data-efficient. The three research authors then co-founded **General Intuition** (see #20).

20. **General Intuition** (NYC, founded Oct 2024, spun out of **Medal**, a gameplay-clip-sharing platform) — CEO Pim de Witte (Medal founder) reportedly **turned down a $500M OpenAI acquisition offer for Medal's dataset** and spun out this lab instead. $134M seed (Oct 2025, Khosla Ventures + General Catalyst + Raine) → **$320M Series A (Jan 2026) at $2.3B valuation** (total disclosed funding $454M) — one of the fastest seed-to-$2B+ valuation jumps in this whole catalog. Investors include Jeff Bezos, Eric Schmidt, Nico Rosberg, and DeepMind/MIT researchers personally. Trains on Medal's "billions of gameplay clips/year" as a uniquely dense action-labeled video dataset. Shipped **MIRA** — a playable multiplayer world model (built with Kyutai + Epic Games) trained on 10K hours of Rocket League data, real-time 20fps, 4-player. Framed explicitly as spatial-temporal reasoning infra for **robotics**, not just gaming — gaming data is the training substrate, embodied AI is the target market.

21. **Decart** (Israel, founded late 2023 by Dean Leitersdorf + Moshe Shalev) — Built **Oasis**, the first interactive "open-world" neural model (went viral, drew huge usage in days). Three product lines powered by shared **DOS** inference-optimization stack: DOS (1,600+ tokens/sec, 8x industry average), **Lucy** (world model for immersive experiences/live video, 1080p/30fps), **Oasis** (world model for physical AI/robotics — pivoted from gaming into autonomous-vehicle driving-simulation sales since June 2026). Profitable within 3 months of launch. Funding trajectory: $21M seed (Sequoia) → $32M more within 4 months → $100M at $3.1B valuation (Aug 2025) → **$300M at ~$4B valuation (May 2026)**, backers include Nvidia, Adobe Ventures, Toyota Ventures, Amazon (strategic customer), Andrej Karpathy (angel). **As of late Aug 2026, in advanced acquisition talks with Anthropic for ~$6-7B** (mostly stock, Anthropic's first Israel R&D center), reportedly beating out a higher NVIDIA bid. If it closes, this would be the largest exit in this entire catalog and validates "gaming-native world models pivot to robotics/physical AI" as a viable path to frontier-lab-scale outcomes.

22. **World Labs** (founded by Fei-Fei Li + Justin Johnson + Christoph Lassner, stealth-exited Sept 2024 with $230M seed at $1B valuation, a16z-backed) — **Marble** (limited beta Nov 2025, full commercial launch Feb 2026): multimodal world model generating navigable, persistent, editable 3D environments from text/image/video/panorama input. **$1B Series B closed Feb 18 2026** (reports suggested a target $5B valuation; company declined to confirm) — anchored by a **$200M strategic investment from Autodesk** (explicit entertainment + CAD/design use-case partnership), plus AMD, Nvidia, Fidelity, Emerson Collective, Sea. Total raised $1.23B — more capital than Google paid to acquire DeepMind in 2014, raised before any disclosed revenue. Public **World API** (Jan 2026) lets developers embed Marble generation into their own apps. **Atlas** (announced Sept 1 2026, same day as this research) — next-gen "omni" multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer, natively trained on text/image/video/3D jointly, positioned to power future Marble versions; early access only. World Labs also quietly acquired **SceniX** (per a16z podcast reference, July 2026) for robotics-simulation talent — a acqui-hire signal mirroring Decart's own robotics pivot.

23. **Odyssey** (backed by Pixar co-founder Edwin Catmull) — "Interactive video": AI-generated video you watch AND navigate with WASD-style controls, "early Holodeck," explicitly candid that "the experience today feels like exploring a glitchy dream." Runs on H100 clusters, ~40ms per-frame generation claimed. Shipped three progressively more ambitious models in 2026: **Odyssey-2** (general interactive-video API, developer.odyssey.ml), **Starchild-1** (first multimodal world model — synchronized audio+video in real time, 4 interaction modes including "narrator-style companionship"), and **Agora-1** (first **multi-agent** world model — up to 4 players share one generated deathmatch world built on GoldenEye nostalgia, decoupled simulation/rendering architecture, playable now at agora.odyssey.ml). Explicitly analogized by outside technical commentary to "a modern game engine, only learned from data rather than hand-authored."

24. **Tripo AI / VAST** (SF/China, founded early 2023 by Song Yachen, ex-MiniMax co-founder) — Covered in depth under 3D asset gen (#31) but also running **Project Eden**, a world-model research preview explicitly "decoupling underlying state simulation from visual rendering" — a "new paradigm," per the company, distinct from Genie/Odyssey/Decart's more end-to-end approaches. Raised **~$350M across 2026 alone** ($200M Series A+/A++ June 2026 + $150M Series A3 July 2026), backers spanning gaming (Tencent, NetEase-adjacent via VAST's client roster, 4399 Network, Tanwan, Giant Network), automotive (Geely Capital), and finance (Fosun, CoStone, Addor). VAST's own Series A ($50M, co-led by Alibaba + Hengxu Capital) is a separate corporate entity from the Tripo product brand — sources use "Tripo AI" and "VAST" somewhat interchangeably; treat as the same group. 6.5M+ creators, 90,000+ developers on Tripo Studio.

25. **Midjourney** — No standalone game shipped. CEO David Holz has publicly stated the long-term roadmap explicitly targets **real-time open-world "Sims"** as "the final creative medium" (his term), building block-by-block: image → video (V1 Video, June 2026, ~$Nx cost of image gen) → 3D-native asset generation (V7, native to the diffusion architecture, not a bolt-on) → spatial navigation → real-time. Stated 12-18 month timeline (from mid-2025) toward a "full spatial creation suite" — as of Sept 2026 still pre-real-time-world-model. Reportedly ~$600M annual revenue funds this roadmap without external capital (Midjourney has historically been self-funded/profitable, unlike every other company in this section).

26. **Matrix-Game 3.0** (research, Apr 2026 preprint) — Memory-augmented interactive world model, up to 40fps at 720p with a 5B model, minute-long spatiotemporal consistency; scales to a 2×14B configuration. Representative of the dense academic/Chinese-lab research pipeline (unifies Unreal-Engine-style explicit state with learned rendering) feeding the commercial products above — illustrates that the research frontier is moving faster than any single commercial product's public roadmap.

## D. Big-Tech Platform Integrations (the incumbents' own AI-native pivots)

27. **Roblox AI (Cube Foundation Model)** — **Cube 3D** (2025): text-to-static-3D-object generation. **4D Generation** (beta, Feb 2026): adds functional behavior via "schemas" (Car-5 = 5-part drivable car; Body-1 = single-mesh functional object) — players can prompt a car into existence and drive it, in-experience, in real time. Early access drew ~200 creator sign-ups in first weeks (Nov 2025), opened to all creators by 2026. **CubePart** (May 2026): open-vocabulary part-controllable generation — creators define arbitrary part schemas rather than being limited to two presets; trained on 460,000+ assets / 2.02M parts (11x larger than prior public 3D datasets), auto-labeled via VLM pipeline (no manual labeling bottleneck). Roblox's stated end-state: full natural-language scene generation (assets + environments + code + animations) directly in Studio. **This is the most concrete "shipped, at scale, generating real player behavior change" example of generative 3D in this entire catalog** — it's live in a platform with 80M+ daily actives, not a demo.

28. **Epic Games — MetaHuman / Unreal Engine 5.8 / UEFN** — MetaHuman 5.8 (2026): **MetaHuman Collections** (crowds scaling to thousands via Mass + Nanite + Instanced Skinned Meshes), **Mesh-to-MetaHuman** now handles full bodies (previously heads-only), **MetaHuman Animator** now does markerless full-body capture from a single webcam (no mocap rig needed), core **RigLogic/DNA libraries open-sourced under MIT** (OpenRigLogic) — a notable move toward interoperability outside Unreal itself. Separately, **UEFN now natively supports Unreal MCP** (Aug 20 2026) — Claude Code/Cursor-style coding agents can directly write/compile Verse code and manipulate Fortnite islands inside the editor, no plugin install required. This is the single most concrete "AI agents building UGC games autonomously" infrastructure shipped by a major platform to date. Epic's generative AI strategy is notably **procedural/tooling-first, not generative-content-first** — no Epic-branded "prompt a character" feature has shipped; the company is enabling third-party and agentic tools rather than building its own consumer-facing generative NPC/asset product.

29. **Unity AI** — Deprecated **Unity Muse** (Oct 1 2025) in favor of **Unity AI**: in-editor assistant (Ask/Agent/Plan modes, project-context-aware), **AI Gateway** (bring-your-own third-party model, e.g. Claude/GPT), **MCP Server** (official Model Context Protocol server for IDE-driven agents), and **Generators** (sprites/textures/materials/animations/sound from text/image references, all outputs metadata-tagged as AI-generated). **Sentis** (inference engine for running custom models at runtime) persists unchanged under the new branding. Free for Pro/Enterprise/Industry licensees; $10/mo add-on for Personal-tier users — Unity, unlike Roblox, is charging directly for AI access rather than bundling it into platform take-rate.

30. **NetEase** — Not a startup but a major AI-native-game **buyer/integrator**, not builder: invested in **VAST** (Tripo AI) via its Series A, and has shipped VAST's Tripo model **into two live commercial games**: **Egg Party (蛋仔派对)** — UGC 3D model creation via "Egg Boy" feature reduced to "minutes" using Tripo LLM + NetEase's own 3D pipeline (DreamMaker team); and **Where Winds Meet** (launched Dec 2024) — used Tripo AI in production art pipeline. NetEase's own **AI Business team within Interactive Entertainment's Art Design Center** is the internal group driving this. A brokerage research note (CICC, 2026) explicitly argues world models "have not touched the core of the gaming industry" yet — current value is localized (pre-production, 3D asset speed), not yet a full game-design substitute — and recommends NetEase + Take-Two as "AI-empowered incumbents" alongside Roblox + XD Inc. as "ecosystem platforms" to watch, a useful read on how sell-side analysts are currently bucketing the investable landscape.

31. **Krafton — inZOI / Smart Zoi + PUBG Ally** — See NVIDIA ACE (#4) for technical detail. Commercially: **inZOI** sold 1.2-1.5M copies in year one of Early Access (launched Mar 27 2025, $9.99, Unreal Engine 5), generating an estimated ~$35.7M VGI revenue — but concurrent players have "plummeted" since launch per a year-one retrospective, and the studio head has publicly accepted "a few hundred thousand returns" as a cost of the early-access approach, openly stating "we had no choice but to use early access — the users are testing it on our behalf." Full 1.0 release targeted March 2027 (originally implied earlier). **This is the most detailed public "here's what an AI-forward AAA life-sim launch actually looked like, warts and all" case study available** — genuinely useful diligence material given Krafton is a public company (KRX: 259960) reporting real financials, unlike most names in this catalog.

## E. 3D Asset Generation (text/image → game-ready mesh)

32. **Meshy** (founded 2023 by Ethan Hu, MIT PhD in computer graphics) — **Largest single funding round in dedicated AI-3D history**: ~$400M Series B (July 2026) at **$1.5B valuation**, led by IDG Capital, Matrix Partners China, Monolith Management (all-China-linked capital, per TechFundingNews) — flagged explicitly by outside coverage as part of a pattern where "nearly every major AI-3D funding round in 2026...has been backed almost entirely by China-linked capital." 10M+ users claimed. Ships full pipeline (text/image → mesh → PBR texture → auto-rig → 500+ animation presets → export to Unity/Unreal/Godot/Roblox/Blender). Launched **Meshy 3D Agent** (conversational, multi-turn 3D creation), **Smart Topology** (game-ready low-poly geometry in ~10 sec), **8K Texture**, **Auto Split** (multi-part 3D-printing). Customers include Nexon, NetEase Games, 37 Interactive Entertainment (gaming) + Bambu Lab/Creality/Elegoo (3D printing) + Hugo Boss. Claims ~$1/model, ~1-minute generation, cutting a multi-day specialist workflow to near-zero marginal cost — the sharpest "AI collapses a cost center" claim in this whole catalog, if true at scale.

33. **Tripo AI** — see #24 (world models) — also the #2 (by most trackers, #1 by others) commercial 3D-generation platform, in a tight, well-funded rivalry with Meshy. Latest models: **Tripo H3.1** (high structural precision) and **Tripo P1.0** (seconds-fast production meshes), "Smart Mesh" (5K-20K face game-ready topology, no manual retopology), DCC Bridge (real-time Unity/Unreal/Blender push). Partners span Riot, EA, Sony, Tencent, NetEase.

34. **Deemos / Hyper3D Rodin** (Shanghai, founded 2020) — **Rodin Gen-2.5** (May 2026): sculpt-level detail up to 10M+ polygons, 5-tier "thinking effort" quality/speed dial, 3D ControlNet, localized editing, 12K-res native texture model, "think-then-generate" workflow modeled on LLM reasoning patterns. Series A-III (June 23 2026, "several hundred million yuan") led by Cathay Capital + state-backed SSCI Leading Private Equity Fund — notable for direct Chinese state capital participation in a gaming-adjacent AI infra company. ARR reportedly grew 400%+ month-on-month post Gen-2.5 launch; ~80% of revenue from overseas markets (ByteDance, Unity, Figma, Canva are customers) — an unusually export-oriented China AI-infra business.

35. **CSM (Common Sense Machines) — FAILED/ACQUIRED**: Shut down its **Cube** platform Jan 5 2026; company acquired by **Google** the same month, folded into Google DeepMind. Prior 2D-to-3D generation product simply removed from the market with no successor — a second (after PlayAI) confirmed case in 2026 of a funded 3D/AI-gaming infra company disappearing via acqui-hire rather than shutting down for lack of demand.

36. **Kaedim** (SF, founded 2020 by Konstantina Psoma) — **$15.2M total** ($15M Series A led by a16z GAMES, March 2024). **Pivoted away from self-serve/indie pricing entirely** by 2026 — no public pricing page, enterprise-only "talk to us" model with human-in-the-loop QA layered on AI generation (review/markup/iterate/approve). Case studies with Lowe's, SharkNinja, Casio (largely non-gaming e-commerce/product-design), plus "one of the top 10 games of all time" and "one of the top 3 mobile game studios" (unnamed) as customers. 55,000+ models delivered, 207,000+ artist-hours saved claimed across 250+ studios. **The clearest example in this catalog of a company abandoning the self-serve indie-developer market for enterprise-only contracts** — a real signal about unit economics: pure AI-3D-generation-as-a-product apparently doesn't hold up against the human-QA-as-a-service model at indie price points.

37. **Luma AI Genie — SUNSET**: Officially discontinued Jan 1 2026 (no new generations; existing content still exportable). Luma pivoted fully to video generation (Dream Machine) and "creative agents" — a case of a company deprioritizing 3D/gaming to chase the larger video-gen market rather than a failure per se, but functionally the same outcome for game developers who had built around it.

38. **Scenario** (LA, founded 2022) — Style-consistent, custom-LoRA-trained game art generation; API-first + Unity plugin + MCP server. Modest funding vs. Meshy/Tripo: PitchBook shows only **$10.9M raised** (seed $6M Jan 2023 led by Play Ventures, unclear subsequent rounds — funding trackers conflict/are incomplete). 15,000+ customers claimed including Ubisoft, Unity, Scopely, InnoGames. Spans 500+ models from 40+ providers (image/video/3D/audio) rather than building proprietary foundation models — a broker/aggregator positioning distinct from Meshy/Tripo's own-model approach.

39. **PrometheanAI** (LA, founded 2018 by Andrew Maximov, ex-Naughty Dog Technical Art Director on Uncharted) — Environment-design/set-dressing assistant (not asset *generation* — asset *placement and scene composition*), works with any 3D editor (primary Unreal integration, open-source Unity/3ds Max/Maya/Blender plugins). Backed by Disney Accelerator; PlayStation Studios among enterprise users. Notably positions itself as "artist-first," having served as a technical advisor in artist class-action suits against major text-to-image AI companies — an unusual anti-training-data-theft stance from an AI-tools vendor.

41. **Layer AI** (SF, founded 2020, "AI application gateway for game developers") — Multi-modal (2D/3D/video/audio) content pipeline, tool-agnostic ("glue" layer across many AI models rather than one proprietary model) — same aggregator positioning as Scenario, but mobile-studio-focused. **$13.3M total** ($1.8M seed 2023 → $6.5M seed round May 2025 led by Arcadia [Tripledot co-founder Akin Babayigit's fund] + a King co-founder's investment vehicle). 200+ studio customers (Zynga, Tripledot, SciPlay, Huuuge Games, Machine Zone) — heavily mobile-live-ops-games weighted, distinct from Meshy/Tripo's broader entertainment/3D-printing/e-commerce reach.

- **CSM (Common Sense Machines)** — Cube platform shut down Jan 5 2026, acquired by Google/DeepMind same month.

No cause disclosed publicly; game canceled/shelved. A cautionary tale that elite AAA design pedigree + genuinely novel AI-systemic design (not just an AI wrapper) still isn't sufficient against current studio-funding/market conditions.
- **Yield Guild Games — YGG Play sunset** (July 31 2026) — Web3 gaming publisher (LOL Land, Waifu Sweeper) shut its publishing unit after "the 10/10 crash" (crypto market event) destroyed retail psychology; pivoted the entire 1M-person community/guild network into **selling gaming behavioral data to AI labs** ("AI Alerts" job board, 27,000 applications in 5 days) — effectively repositioning an entire Web3-gaming organization as an **AI-training-data-labor supplier**, not a games company at all. ## K. Cost of Real-Time World Models / AI NPCs (compute economics)

- **Inference, not training, now dominates AI compute spend**: industry-wide, inference is ~85% of enterprise AI budget and roughly 2/3 of global AI compute spend as of early 2026 (the "Inference Flip" — cumulative inference spend surpassed cumulative training spend industry-wide in early 2026). Organizations reportedly face **$15-20B in inference costs for every $1B spent on training** over a model's lifecycle.
- **Raw GPU pricing (Apr-Aug 2026, on-demand)**: H100 SXM5 ~$1.99-2.90/hr; H200 SXM5 ~$2.30-4.54/hr; B200 SXM6 ~$2.00-6.00/hr on-demand, spot as low as ~$2.00-2.12/hr. Spot vs. on-demand gaps of 50-70%+ are common (H100 spot as low as $0.80/hr vs. $2.90-2.90 on-demand on some platforms).
- **Cost-per-million-tokens (CPM) varies 5-15x on software decisions alone** (batching config, utilization, quantization/FP8) — bigger levers than which GPU SKU is chosen. Typical enterprise GPU utilization during inference is only **15-30%**, meaning idle-but-billed capacity is often the single largest cost driver, not raw chip price.
- **Direct quote relevant to AI-NPC unit economics** (veriprajna.com, independent technical analysis): cloud AI NPCs can "burn 5 to 30x more tokens" than a simple chat interaction once voice, memory, and multi-turn context are included, at an estimated **$500,000-2,000,000/year** in inference cost for a mid-scale live game — and critically, for free-to-play titles where a small fraction of players monetize, "serving cloud AI to the non-paying majority can erase your margin entirely" — dubbed the "**success tax**": the AI feature's cost scales with exactly the engagement metric studios are trying to maximize.
- **This is the direct explanation for the "on-device SLM" pattern seen across NVIDIA ACE deployments** (PUBG Ally's 2B-param model quantized to run in 8GB VRAM headroom, inZOI's Smart Zoi, Total War's local advisor): shifting inference cost from the studio's cloud bill to the player's already-purchased GPU is the only way several AAA studios have found to make always-on AI NPCs unit-economically viable at scale. Cloud-hosted platforms (Inworld, Convai, Charisma.ai) remain structurally exposed to this "success tax" unless/until they also push more inference on-device — Inworld's own on-device runtime is proprietary/closed, a real lock-in/pricing-power lever for them but also a real cost-of-adoption tax on developers.
- **World-model-specific compute is even heavier than NPC dialogue**: Decart's DOS claims 1,600+ tokens/sec (8x "industry average") specifically as an optimization sell — implying the un-optimized baseline for interactive video/world generation is dramatically more expensive per unit of user-facing output than text-only NPC dialogue. Odyssey explicitly frames its own current output as "expensive at first, but sooner than you'd think, something everyone will be able to use" — i.e., even the vendors admit today's real-time world-model economics don't yet support mass consumer deployment outside a research-preview/enterprise-API framing.

## L. UGC Platforms as Distribution (Roblox, Fortnite/UEFN, Minecraft)

- **Roblox** is functionally becoming the default distribution + monetization layer for generative 3D content aimed at end consumers: Cube/4D-Gen/CubePart ship directly inside Roblox Studio, meaning any indie creator (not just AI-native startups) gets access to frontier 3D generation for free, bundled into the platform they already use to reach an 80M+ DAU audience and take Roblox's existing revenue-share economics. This directly threatens the addressable market for standalone AI-3D-asset startups selling *into* Roblox-adjacent workflows (though most, e.g. Meshy/Tripo, also sell direct exports to Roblox Studio as one of many supported engines, hedging this risk).
- **Fortnite / UEFN**: Epic's Aug 20 2026 native Unreal MCP support inside UEFN is the most direct "agentic AI can now build a shippable UGC game with almost no custom tooling" moment in this catalog — any Claude Code/Cursor-class coding agent can read/write/compile Verse and manipulate the Scene Graph directly. Combined with Fortnite's **100% creator V-Bucks retention through 2026** and engagement-based payouts, this makes UEFN arguably the single most attractive near-term distribution surface for an AI-agent-built game studio to test a Monastery-style bet on "one AI-native builder + agentic tooling = disproportionate output" — the incentive structure (retention %, existing audience, native agent tooling) all point the same direction simultaneously, right now.
- **Minecraft (Java/Bedrock + Minecraft Education)**: no equivalent native generative-AI content-creation pipeline has shipped from Microsoft/Mojang directly (contrast with Roblox/Epic); most Minecraft-side generative-AI activity is either (a) third-party mods/agents (Player2's original pitch was "AI Player from vision input" inside Minecraft; Altera's Project Sid ran its 1,000-agent civilization simulation in Minecraft specifically because it's open, scriptable, and well-documented for agent research) or (b) Microsoft's own world-model research (WHAM/Muse) being trained on a *different* first-party title (Bleeding Edge), not Minecraft itself. Minecraft's main 2026 collaboration news is infrastructure (cross-tenant multiplayer for schools), not generative content — Minecraft Education is covered in more depth in the parallel education research stream, not duplicated here.
- **Common thread**: the platforms with (1) an existing massive creator base, (2) native low/no-code tooling, and (3) favorable creator economics (Roblox rev-share, Fortnite's 100% V-Bucks retention) are absorbing generative-AI capability *as a platform feature* faster than standalone AI-native game startups can build independent distribution — a structural headwind for any Monastery bet that is "a generative game/NPC tool" without its own wedge into (or explicit non-reliance on) one of these three platforms.
