## PART I — FLAGSHIP & MARKET CONTEXT

### One Medical / Amazon Health
- **Type**: A2 — Hybrid telehealth + in-person primary care  
- **Acquired**: Amazon acquired One Medical for $3.9B in Feb 2023  
- **Scale**: 200+ locations, 20+ US cities; 10,000+ employer contracts; Prime member discount ($99/yr vs $199/yr standard)  
- **Recent**: 2026 reorganized AHS into 6 business units; GLP-1 management program launched; AI chatbot in app; kiosks dispensing medications at clinic sites; expanded NJ, Cleveland, Westchester via health system partnerships (Hackensack Meridian, Montefiore, Cleveland Clinic)  
- **Model**: Membership ($99–$199/yr) + insurance-billed visits; pharmacy integration; tech-enabled scheduling/messaging  
- **Revenue**: Amazon does not disclose separately. Strong growth reported; "revenue reinvention" per SVP  
- **Monastery filter**: Not investable — PE/public company asset. Benchmark for what "works" at scale.  
- **Source**: AHA June 2025; CoStar 2026; Amazon Health.amazon.com

### Forward Health — ❌ DEAD (Nov 2024)
- **Type**: A1/A3 → failure  
- **Raised**: $650M+ total; Series E $100M (Nov 2023)  
- **Shutdown**: Nov 12, 2024 — abrupt closure of all locations, app disabled, ~200 employees lost jobs  
- **Cause**: Bet on CarePod (AI kiosks in malls/gyms); planned 3,200 in 12 months, deployed only 5; funding round on table but couldn't bridge; CEO Adrian Aoun called it "swing for the fences" moment  
- **Failure mode**: Hardware complexity + reactive care limitations + scaling infrastructure cost; consumer acquisition too hard; $225M in 2021 → expanded too fast  
- **Post-mortem**: Aoun immediately started new venture; assets (CarePod IP) up for grabs  
- **Lesson**: Kiosk-first primary care with no physician relationship can't scale. Physical hardware + autonomous diagnosis without human touchpoint = liability + churn  
- **Source**: Business Insider, FierceHealthcare, Beckers ASC — Nov 2024
