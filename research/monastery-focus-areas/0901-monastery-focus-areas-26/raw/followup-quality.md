## 1. Higgsfield $700M ARR — verified as *self-reported run-rate*, not GAAP

- **Claim origin**: Higgsfield PR Newswire, 2026-08-17, Series B $400M at $5.4B led by DST Global; "this month, the company also reached $700 million in annualized revenue." Also in TechCrunch 2026-08-17 and Reuters/BNN Bloomberg.
- **What it is**: founder/company run-rate, 16 months after web launch, up from ~$20M a year earlier (The Next Web). Multiple ~7.7x ARR.
- **What it is not**: audited FY revenue. TNW explicitly: "If those customers are still there in a year, the run rate becomes revenue. If the churn shows up first, $700mn was a peak rather than a floor."
- **GP use**: acceptable as evidence that **B2B/prosumer generative video production** (ads, marketing, studio tools) is where money is. **Do not** treat as proof of consumer generative entertainment PMF. Higgsfield is a production-tool company, not Generative Netflix.
